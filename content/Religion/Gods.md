>*Beings given faith and power by mortals. Some good, some bad, some neutral, depending on your point view.  All still bound to Lord Ao. The Time of Troubles revealed that much, but it also revealed something far worse. Mortals becoming gods.*

The gods of this world are far and many, with many gods giving power to their followers. The Faerûnian pantheon is the most worshiped pantheon on [[Faerûn]]. Pantheons were a group of deities who were worshiped by people who shared one characteristic, for example sharing the same cultural or racial background. In the Faerûnian pantheon's case the believers' shared characteristic was a geographic one, people who lived in those parts of the continent [[Faerûn]] where other pantheons did not hold sway were the believers.
