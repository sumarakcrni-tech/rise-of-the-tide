>_The Forest Father is the eternal guardian of the forest. He is the god of all wild and untamed nature throughout [[Faerûn]]. Priests and druids of the realms call him Silvanus, though he is known by many names._

Silvanus is the Faerunian god representing power of wild nature standing in contrast to the more ordered, agrarian nature represented by the goddess [[Chauntea]].
