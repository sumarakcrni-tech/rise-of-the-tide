**Mask** is the intermediate god of shadows, thievery, and formerly intrigue in the Faerûnian pantheon, a loner god most often associated with those of ill-repute. The Lord of Shadows is a chaotic neutral or neutral evil deity symbolized with a velvet black mask tainted with red.
