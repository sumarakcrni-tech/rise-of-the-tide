>*..I choose to rule for all eternity as the ultimate tyrant. I can induce hatred and strife at my whim, and all will bow down before me while in my kingdom.*

**Bane** is the Faerunian god of tyrannical oppression, terror, and hate, known across [[Faerûn]] as the face of pure evil through malevolent despotism. From his dread plane of Banehold, The Black Hand acted indirectly through worshipers and other agents to achieve his ultimate plan to achieve total domination of all [[Faerûn]].
