>*Nature is balance. Balance is life. Nature is life. I am nature. I am life.*

**Silvan**, or **The Leshen, The Mesmer in Northern** is an old deity. Long forgotten by the pantheon of the [[Gods]], Silvan was once the only ruler of the Feywild, without the Summer and Winter Court. He represents nature unbound and unrestrained. The [[Gods]] were terrified of that prospect, so they sought to banish him to Shadowfell. After millennia, Silvan was once again awoken by The [[Spellplague]], deep within Shadowfell. He now seeks to restore his rightful claim to the Feywild, and ending the Courts once and for all.
