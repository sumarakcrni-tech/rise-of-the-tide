>*I guard this path! Do not approach. This way is forbidden...*

**Helm** is the Faerûnian god of guardians and protectors, worshiped by guards and paladins both. The Vigilant One had long been seen as a cold and focused deity who impartially took the role of defender, and sometimes enforcer, but his activities in the Time of Troubles caused the folk of [[Faerûn]] to view him differently. The Watcher was the epitome of the vigilant guardian and embodiment of the spirit of watchfulness, whether used for good or ill. In 1486 DR, he returned to [[Faerûn]] with the restored faith of his chosen.
