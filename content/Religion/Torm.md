>*Men call me Torm the True because I value loyalty above all else. They call me Torm the Brave because I will face any danger to prove my respect of duty. Any who would call himself my follower must do the same.* 

**Torm** is the Faerûnian patron deity of paladins and the god of duty, loyalty, righteousness, obedience and, after the [[Spellplague]], law and valor. By the late 15th century DR, Torm became the war god of courage and self-sacrifice.
