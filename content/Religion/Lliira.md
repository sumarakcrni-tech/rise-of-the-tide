>*The arrow shot, sweet triumph strikes she home*  
*Into the breast of heroes, who no more shall roam.*  
*To the winds fly wits – ambition o'er leaps the stars!*  
*Our court we pay to Lliira – not to [[Shar]]!*

Lliira is a chaotic good lesser deity of joy (and for a time an exarch of [[Sune]]) in the Faerûnian pantheon. Everchanging and always lively, she is the embodiment of happiness and freedom, inspiring poets, musicians, and all who reveled in the wonder of a free, cheerful life. The Joybringer is the archetypal ingénue dancer, showing by example that few things should draw one away from the Elysian Rigadoon, a philosophy that prioritized above all things the act of joyful movement.
