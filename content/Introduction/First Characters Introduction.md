# The Beginning
 
The year is 1489 Dale Reckoning. Following the battle between the [Netherese](Netherese) and [Bedine](Bedine.md) for control of [Anuaroch](Anauroch), the newly awakened [Phaerimm](Phaerimm) magically drain the region, turning it into a desert. The faithful prayers to [[gods]] for understanding and mercy appear to go unheard, sending messages only to their Chosen. The nation of [Sembia](Sembia.md), now free from the [Netherese](Netherese) grasp, prospers with mercantile, sending merchant caravans to travel across all of Faerun. [The Flaming Rose Company](The%20Flaming%20Rose%20Company) has hired a group of adventures to join one of the caravans as guards, headed for the city of [Triboar](Triboar). But the road to [Triboar](Triboar) is long and perilous with bandits oft roaming about. The caravan stops for the night near a river, setting up camp. This is where our story begins. Or at least, it did.
