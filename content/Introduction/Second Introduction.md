Nestled underneath [[The High Forest]], a small village of [Hoghaven](My%20Notebook/Settlements/Hoghaven.md) resides. It is famous for its world renowned hogs that were so tasty, even the conservative Lords of Waterdeep adore them. Recently, the village had started to see its fair share of visitors and traders, something the conservative villagers do not appreciate. Amongst one of these visitors, a strange group of "would-be" adventurers appeared on the doorstep of [Hoghaven](My%20Notebook/Settlements/Hoghaven.md). What will their story be?
