>*We were forced into places unseen by others, all because we showed true mortal nature.*

The Shadovar, sometimes pluralized as Shadovars, were the inhabitants of Thultanthar, also known as the City of Shade. During the course of the people's 1700-year exile from Toril, they battled the malaugrym and other shadowy horrors of the Plane of Shadow, before returning in the late 14th century DR and reestablishing a [[Netherese]] presence in [[Faerûn]].
