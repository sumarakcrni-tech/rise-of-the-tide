>The Netherese, also known as the Neth, are an ethnic group of dark-haired, fair-skinned [[Human]] that resided in the ancient magocratic nation of [Netheril](Netheril). Many cultures and ethnic groups in [[Faerûn]] were either descendants or absorbed into the Netherese people, such as the Shadovar, the Halruaans, the [[Bedine]], and the [[Illuskans]], among others.

## Description
 
 Only a few select groups of pure-blood Netherese still existed on Toril as of the 14th century DR, over a thousand years after the fall of the great Empire of Magic. The purebloods were an ethnic group of dark-haired, fair-skinned [[Human]] that resided in the ancient magocratic nation of [Netheril](Netheril).

## Culture
The Netherese had always lived in a hierarchical magocracy, that was seperated into the flying enclaves of High [[Netheril]] and the cities of Low [[Netheril]] located around the Narrow Sea. While the people shared a common script of draconic alphabet, the people of Low [[Netheril]] spoke Netherese while the nobles of High [[Netheril]] spoke Loross. The reason for two languages was due to separation of the classes.

## Politics
Netherese considered themselves above everyone else on [[Faerûn]] and were generally hostile to other races, frequently taking slaves for magical experiments and hard labor. Following the fall of the High [[Netheril]] in the 4th century before Dale Reckoning, the survivors of three cities of High [[Netheril]], Anauria, Asram, and Hlondath, along with the commoners of Low [[Netheril]] spread out from northern [[Faerûn]] across the rest of the realms. The enclave of Thultanthar managed to survive the cataclysmic destruction by magically shifting into the Shadowfell.

## Religion
The Netherese pantheon was composed of ten deities. After the fall of [[Netheril]], most of the deities died, vanished, or came to be known by different names. Four goddesses within the Netherese pantheon represented the four seasons.
