### Description

- Erethas is a 179 year old male high elf arcanist.
- He has silver eyes and wavy, silver hair.
- He has smooth skin.
- He stands 197 cm tall and has a gaunty build.
- He has a round, beautiful face.
- He is missing his right arm and has a prosthetic.

### Traits

- He worships Silvan and is determined to bring the world back to its natural order (Lawful Evil)
- He is very loud and boisterous.
- He feels most comfortable in nature.
- He sporadically thinks aloud.
- He is an arcane master for his young age.

### Relationship

- Widow.

### Voice

- Floaty, mid-toned voice with a smartass undertone.

### Plot Hook

- Erethas' name is brought up several times, once during the party's interaction with the elves of Selren Themar and once when they find a wooden necklace with a stag skull in front of a tree on a strange creature near Secomber. Erethas is Dilath's brother.
 
# **P****lot Points**

**B****ackstory**  
_E__rethas' story begins a long time ago, back when he was but a child living in Shyll Alora located in the High Forest. His days were mostly spent in learning about the history and religion as well as intricate spell weaving. The child became an afficionado on the arcane and was on the right track to becoming a minister in his later years. But all of that changed one day. After being sent by the Archwizard of Shyll Alora to explore the nearby ruins, Erethas and his party stumbled upon a wondrous place of power underneath the ruins. A magnificent tree inscribed with runes, telling a tale of nature and unparalled beauty, of nature unbound. Erethas was mesmerized with this sight. He reported it to the Archwizard, but the leader was sceptical of it. He kept repeating how the Summer Court and the Winter Court hold nature in balance. Erethas listened to his words carefully, but his heart knew better. A couple of days later, Erethas felt an urge to visit the ruins again, but this time he would go alone. He navigated his way to the great tree and observed the runes on it. Suddenly, a vision of a great deer skull pierced his mind, and a serene voice spoke to him. Erethas was once again mesmerized in nature's beauty. A violent crash brought him back to his senses. Another crash was heard above ground. He rushed outside only to find his home being sieged by machines of war. He took out his spellcasting focus, but something jumped him from behind and closed his mouth before he could utter a phrase. The world started spinning and he was gone. The next time he woke up, he only saw ash and death from the place that was once his home. On that day, he swore that he would bring back the natural order and put a stop to these machines of war!_
 
**Current Timeline**  
_E__rethas found himself being consumed more and more each day with visions from the tree. He interpreted them in his own ways, leading him down a dark path. He dreamt of dragons, of cleansing fire, of rebirth and regrowth. Firstly, he formed a mercenary company called_ **The White Blades** _and fought for the highest bidder, waging war in the North, by traditional means. He was an effective leader and his arcane skills proved to be a great asset on the battlefield._
 
_After amassing a vast sum of gold, he made his home in Orogoth and the White Blades were disbanded. However, the mercenaries were made to serve him. Erethas' dreams were becoming even more clearer as he was taught ways how to control his forces through arcane arts. He constructed necklaces for his men to wear, all with the symbol of the deer skull standing in front of a great tree. Erethas realized that after some time, the men started transforming into wildland creatures, the same ones he dreamt of. His dream of an unbound nature would soon be fulfilled._
 
_During his time in Orogoth villa, Erethas came upon a corpse of a black dragon in the caverns below it. The dream spoke to him about ways of ressurecting it, but he was interrupted by a small force of elves from Selren Themar. They were unable to defeat Erethas and his wildland army and became his prisoners. He implanted memories of Selren Themar being under constant attack. Their minds were broken and put under his control, weaving a spell into the lower jaw of their helmets, as punishment._
 
_At one point, Erethas visited Secomber, posing as a merchant, selling the necklaces used to control his men, for scientific research._
 
_Erethas is Dilath's brother._
