### Description

- Denfer is a male lightfoot [[halfling]] brewer.
- He has short, wavy, black hair and blue eyes.
- He has smooth brown skin.
- He stands 106cm (3'5") tall and has a regular build.
- He has a triangular, unremarkable face.
- He has a light hunch.
- He had a magical rune on his chest and the party has identified it being a rune on mind control placed on him by [[Netherese]] magic.
- He is currently in isolation until the party identifies a way to remove the rune.

### Traits

- He discretely worships [[Selûne]].
- He acts on impulse.
- He is very obstinate.
- He disregards poorer people.
- He intermittently thinks aloud.

### Relationships

- Straight, Single.
