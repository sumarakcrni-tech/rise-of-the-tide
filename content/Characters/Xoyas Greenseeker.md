### Description

- Xoyas is a male lightfoot halfling blacksmith and the leader of the Iron Eater Smithy.
- He has long, blue mohawk and green eyes.
- He has rugged pink skin.
- He stands 119cm tall and has a regular build.
- He has a round, slightly attractive face.
- He is a tinkerer at heart and always experiments.
- He particularly enjoys Grom's company with whom he can share his tinkering ideas, or insanities, without any issues.
- He is obsessed with a beverage from the Inn.

### Traits

- He quietly worships [[Mystra]]
- He is a very good diplomat and always works towards resolution of conflict.
- He is very slow to trust other people.
- He stretches the truth to tell a good story.

### Relationships
- Works with [[Dimbos Bavarax]] and [[Rhahmur Sorsk]]
- Very fond of [[Grom]]
