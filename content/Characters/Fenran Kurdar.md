### Description

- Fenran is a male high [[elf]] sorcerer.
- He has steely eyes and long, black hair.
- He has rugged, fair skin.
- He stands 187 cm tall and has a lean build.
- He has a square, slightly fanciable face.
- He has a scar on his left hand.
- Fenran is a male high [[elf]] sorcerer who was found in High Moor in the [[Ruins of Gloom]].
- He and his sister were being controlled by Erethas and forced to relive the trauma of the[[Fall of Selren Thelmar]].
- He and his sister actively help the party in getting rid of [[Mairgesh]] and are searching for a way to stop Erethas.
- After the party rescues him and the other Shad'rai members, he decides to join the scouting division of [[Secomber]] and serves as a medic in their headquarters. He is a also a capable fighter who can hold his own.

### Traits

- He discreetly worships [[Sune]].
- He is a very good diplomat and always works towards resolution of conflict.
- He is very obstinate.
- He sporadically thinks aloud.
- He believes in soulmates.

### Relationships

- [[Liarel Kurdar]] is his sister.
