>*Fresh meat.*

### Description
- Rulf Redstream was a male human cook and butcher.
- He had very long, straight, black hair and brown eyes.
- He had rough, sunburned, red skin.
- He stood 177cm tall and had a lean build.
- He had a square face.
- Rulf was one of the people responsible for the [[Fall of Hoghaven]], chasing [[Luxon]] with a cleaver after [[Luxon]] leapt through the window of the [[Hoghaven]] Tavern to avoid him.
- He assaulted the party during their encounter with [[Mairgesh]] and [[Laila Farlight]].
- The party was defeated and he was not seen again, that is, until he appeared with draconic powers during the [[Bell Incident]] inside **[[Secomber]]**.
- He was captured in the Bell Tower and after he was deemed unnecessary, **[[Elizabeth Laughingsteel]]** shot him with her bow.

### Traits
- Ruthless and obsessed with killing Luxon

### Relationships
- Known to have worked with [[Henry Serpentwind]] and [[Rulf Redstream]].
- Executed by [[Elizabeth Laughingsteel]].
