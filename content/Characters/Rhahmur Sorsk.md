### Description

 - Rhahmur is a male [[human]] blacksmith working in Iron Eater Smithy under [[Xoyas Greenseeker]].
- He has no hair and brown eyes with a glorious manly moustache.
- He has rough brownish skin.
- He stands 182cm tall and has a round build.
- He has a square, average face.
- He is allergic to elves, or so he says.
- He actively helps [[Grom]] with his smithing insanities, such as creating what would be called a rifle in modern terms.

### Traits

- He quietly worships [[Selûne]]
- He is very focused.
- He is slightly lazy.
- He argues about everything.

### Relationships

- Very fond of [[Grom]] and [[Talia Wyndall]].
- Works with [[Dimbos Bavarax]] and [[Xoyas Greenseeker]]
