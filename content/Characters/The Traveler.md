> *The weary traveler grows close to the end of the path.*

### Description

- The Traveler is a mechanical entity that appeared near the machine in the **Library** after the party interacted with the **Arcana Obscura**.
- He has a companion, a magical book called Ithlyx that is sentient.
- He told the party to get the machine working and then faded into thin air, alongside his companion.
