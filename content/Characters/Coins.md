### Description
- Coins is a male [[Kenku]] blacksmith.
- He has brown eyes.
- He has rough white feathers.
- He stands 164cm tall and has a round build.
- He has a white beak.
- He has slightly long fingers.
- He was one of the survivors of the [[Fall of Hoghaven]] and escaped with the party to [[Secomber]] where he now works as a blacksmith, aiding the party.


### Traits
- He is not a typical [[Kenku]] and seems to be very fond of [[Celorfin Garaitnu]]
- Very generous and always willing to help
- He is branded on his neck as a slave with a mark from [[The Flaming Rose Company]].

### Relationships
- Fond of [[Grom]] and [[Azlyn Bennett]]
