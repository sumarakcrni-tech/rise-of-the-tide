### Description
- Werola is an older [[gnome]] lady.
- She has gray bangs and green eyes and was working as librarian in [[Hoghaven]].
- She has smooth, brown skin.
- She stands 71cm tall and has an athletic build.
- She has a soft, average face.
- She escaped with the party during the [[Fall of Hoghaven]] and is currently continuing her work as a librarian in [[Secomber]], alongside [[Luxon]], [[Olympia Skyriver]] and [[Melbor Jahre]].
- She is currently translating the [[Arcana Obscura]], the book she hired the party to retrieve back in [[Hoghaven]].
- She actively aids the party in their endeavors.

### Traits
- Knowledgeable and very helpful to the party

### Relationships
- Very fond of [[Luxon]] and [[Grom]]
- Works with  [[Olympia Skyriver]] and [[Melbor Jahre]]
