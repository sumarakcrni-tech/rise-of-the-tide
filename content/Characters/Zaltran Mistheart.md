### Description

- Zaltran is a 70 year old male lightfoot [[halfling]] astrologer.
- He has cropped, curled, gray hair and green eyes.
- He has rough, pockmarked, golden skin.
- He stands 99cm tall and has a beefy build.
- He has a round, common face with a short beard.
- He has a low-toned voice.
- The first thing he does is greets our adventurers as cleaners.  
- He promised them 200 gold if they retrieve the astrolabe.  
- He mentions the Book of the Dragon in his house.
- He is also the owner of the Iron Hawk, a newspaper published in [[Secomber]].
- He actively helps the party with providing information as well as offers support in eliminating the threat that [[Mairgesh]] poses to [[Secomber]].
- He also sent the party to retrieve a [[Glimmering Astrolabe]], one of the mechanisms needed to operate the strange machine in the Iron Eater Compound library.
- Currently unknown whereabouts.

### Traits

- He discretely worships [[Oghma]].
- He is very focused.
- He reacts violently to feathers.

### Relationships

- He is one of the board members of Iron Eater Compound alongside [[Bronir Fusehorn]] and [[Elizabeth Laughingsteel]].
