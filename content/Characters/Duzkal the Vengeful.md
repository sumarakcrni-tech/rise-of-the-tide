>*The vengeful boar has been tamed.*

Duzkal the Vengeful is an amalgamation of the restless spirits of boars that were slaughtered in [[Hoghaven]] possessed by a spirit of vengeance. The shadowy creature now stalks the lands between [[Hoghaven]], [[the High Forest]] and [[Secomber]], mercilessly exacting vengeance on the local populace.
 
The spirit of Vengeance that resided within the amalgamation was none other than Blob, an orc warrior whose fury was so mighty that his spirit refused to die, thus becoming a spirit of vengeance.

Duzkal has been used and equipped with cannons by [[Grom]] during [[Dilath's Downfall]] and aided massively during the encounter.
