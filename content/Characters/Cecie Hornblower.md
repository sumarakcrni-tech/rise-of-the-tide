### Description

- Cecie Hornblower is a female lightfoot [[halfling]] baker.
- She has long, curled, brown hair and cyan eyes.
- She has smooth white skin.
- She stands 84cm (2'9") tall and has a lean build.
- She has an edgy, cute face.
- She twitches constantly from her back.

### Traits

- She discretely worships [[Tempus]].
- She always has a backup plan.
- She is very pessimistic.
- She tries to stay home as much as possible.
- She doesn't like her profession, has always dreamed of becoming a cook, but is only good at baking.

### Relationships

- Married.
- Resides in The Green in [[Secomber]].
