### Description
 Leo Cartwright was a male [[human]] soldier working in the Constabulary.
- He had short, straight, dyed black hair and blue eyes.
- He had soft white skin.
- He stood 182cm tall and had an anorexic build.
- He had an edgy, common face with a short sideburns.
- He was slaughtered by an unknown [[dragonborn]] in the [[Hoghaven]] Inn during the [[Fall of Hoghaven]].

### Traits
- He appeared well mannered and willing to help, but albeit a bit confused.
