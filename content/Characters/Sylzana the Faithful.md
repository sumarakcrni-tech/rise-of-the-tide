>*Nature is balance. Balance is life. In [[Silvan]], we are one.*

### Description

- Sylzana is a female wood [[elf]] that worships [[Silvan]].
- She has long green hair and blue eyes.
- She has brown, bark skin.
- She stands 172cm tall and has a lean build.
- She has an narrow face.
- She is very graceful.
- She interacted with Luxon and Talia and spoke to them of [[Silvan]] and their motto which was repeated by the other elven sisters in **[[Eyelsera]]**.
- She is the leader of [Eyelsera](Eyelsera.md).
- Sylzana informs the party about missing wood elves in the [High Forest](The%20High%20Forest.md). A scouting party noticed a strange caravan going through the forest and moving north. A couple of days later, strange markings started appearing on the trees.

### Traits

- She proudly worships [[Silvan]].
- She always has a backup plan.
- She will always prioritize her needs.
- She feels most comfortable while surrounded by nature.


### Relationships

- Interacted with [[Luxon]] and [[Talia Wyndall]].
