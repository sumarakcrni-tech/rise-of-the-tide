### Description

- Tharkar Barrelchin is a female hill [[dwarf]] jeweler residing in her shop in The Green.
- She has long, curled, brown hair shaved on the left side and brown eyes.
- She has soft golden skin.
- She stands 104cm (3'4") tall and has a regular build.
- She has a sharp, common face.
- She has a large abstract tattoo on her left hand, a badly done tattoo of a spider on her left leg and a abstract tattoo on her left arm.
- She has a slight lisp.
- The party tried to reunite her with her dead husband for two sapphires, but nothing came of it. The party did identify that her husband was murdered by his brother, [[Harmand Barrelchin]].
- She was raising an Otyugh in [[Secomber]].


### Traits

- She rarely thinks ahead.
- She frequently hums old dwarven songs.
- She occasionally thinks aloud.

### Relationships

- Recently widowed.
