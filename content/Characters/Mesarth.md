### Description
- Mesarth is a male [[dragonborn]] acolyte that worked in the [[Hoghaven]] Church as a priest.
- He has a dark frill and blue eyes.
- He has ridged white scales.
- He stands 202cm tall and has an athletic build.
- He has a diamond-shaped face.
- He is branded on his neck as a slave with a mark from [[The Flaming Rose Company]].
- He was one of the survivors of the [[Fall of Hoghaven]] and escaped with the party to [[Secomber]] where he now works as a medic, aiding the party.

### Traits
- Religious, follows the teachings of [[Bahamut]]
- Always willing to help and aid those in need

### Relationships
- Fond of the survivors from [[Hoghaven]]
- Recently started teaching [[Karstan the Scarred]] about Draconic.
- Interacted with [[Azlyn Bennett]] regarding [[dragonborn]].
