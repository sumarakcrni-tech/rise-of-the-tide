### Description
- Cedric is a [[half-elf]] male farmer.
- He has cropped, braided, brown hair and red eyes.  
- He has rugged white skin.  
- He stands 180cm tall and has a beefy build.  
- He has a square, ordinary face.  
- He often voices his despise for the aristocracy.  
- He likes animals more than people.  
- He used to be a farmer before the [[Fall of Hoghaven]] and escaped with the party to [[Secomber]], where he now works as a waiter in the Iron Eater Tavern.
- Developed an affinity for alcohol.

### Traits
- Very secluded and usually keeps to himself.
- He was infected with Milk Pox.

### Relationships
- Dislikes both [[Gili Hardcloak]] and his wife for trying to set him up with a crime.
