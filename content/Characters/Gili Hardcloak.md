### Description

- Gili Hardcloak is a male hill [[dwarf]] farmer.  
- He has very long, straight, dyed orange hair and green eyes.  
- He has veiny gray skin.  
- He stands 132cm tall and has a beefy build.  
- He has an oval, ordinary face with a long spotty beard.
- Head of the Hardcloak Family.
- He interacted with the party before the [[Fall of Hoghaven]] and was nowhere to be seen after it.  
- His status is unknown.

### Traits

- He worships [[Tempus]].
- He easily holds grudges.

### Relationships

- In dispute with [[Cedric Cleves]].
