- Vadrey is a female stout [[halfling]] cook working in Iron Eater Tavern.
- She has cropped, wavy, gray hair and gray eyes.
- She has smooth brown skin.
- She stands 106cm (3'5") tall and has a lean build.
- She has an oval, sightly face.
- She has a scar on her right arm.
- She told the party about [[Denfer Teacloak]] and his magical rune.

### Traits

- She is always serene.
- She intermittently uses long words to sound smart.
- She knows all the gossip around town.

### Relationships
