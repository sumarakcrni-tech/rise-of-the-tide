>*Behind his eyes, something else lurks. Something sinister.*

### Description

- Henry Serpentwind was a male [[human]] barrister.
- He had long, curly, black hair shaved on the left side and blue eyes.
- He had rugged, pockmarked, brown skin.
- He stood 175cm tall and had a round build.
- He had a square, creepy face with a medium sideburns.
- Henry was one of the people responsible for the [[Fall of Hoghaven]].
- He assaulted the party during their encounter with [[Mairgesh]], [[Laila Farlight]] and [[Rulf Redstream]].
- The party was defeated and Henry was not seen again, that is, until he appeared with draconic powers during the [[Bell Incident]] inside [[Secomber]].
- He was sent flying through the window by [[Grom]] after he punched him inside the Bell Tower and died from the fall.

### Traits
- Relentless and cruel

### Relationships
- Worked with [[Mairgesh]], [[Rulf Redstream]] and [[Laila Farlight]]
- Responsible for the party being defeated by [[Mairgesh]].
- The party got their revenge with [[Grom]] delivering the final blow.
