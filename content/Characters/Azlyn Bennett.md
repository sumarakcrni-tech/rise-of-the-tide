**What Azlyn discovers:**  
_As your eyes close, you dream of snow and blood. You raise your head and see the face of a man, staring back at you angrily. A tattoo of a thunderbeast graces his chest. His voice booms and you are knocked back onto the snow. The man grabs you and starts dragging you through the blood-covered snow. He picks you up by your tiny leg and tosses you into the forest. But you do not cry. A warrior of Uthardt does not cry. You try and return to the man, but he threatens you with an axe. "No daughter of mine is fit to be a shaman! Begone! Lest I cut your throat meself." You take another step forward, and the man lunges at you. Your eyes wake up from this nightmare._
 
Azlyn discovers that the Thunderbeast tribe is led by Harthhulk Hornspear, her biological father. The citizens of Beliard were the victims of the latest raid, in the Dessarin Valley.
 
Azlyn also discovers that the way to gain favor from Uthgar is to slay monsters in battle, specifically griffons, pegasi and treants.
 
By speaking with [[Mesarth]], Azlyn discovers that the dragonborn look very much like dragons standing erect in humanoid form, though they lack wings or a tail. The first dragonborn had scales of vibrant hues matching the colors of their dragon kin, but generations of interbreeding have created a more uniform appearance. Their small, fine scales are usually brass or bronze in color, sometimes ranging to scarlet, rust, gold, or copper-green. They are tall and strongly built, often standing close to 6½ feet tall and weighing 300 pounds or more. Their hands and feet are strong, talonlike claws with three fingers and a thumb on each hand.
 
The blood of a particular type of dragon runs very strong through some dragonborn clans. These dragonborn often boast scales that more closely match those of their dragon ancestor—bright red, green, blue, or white, lustrous black, or gleaming metallic gold, silver, brass, copper, or bronze. Each dragonborn is capable of using their breath as a weapon, based on their ancestry. Black and copper use acid, blue and bronze lightning, red, brass and gold use fire, silver and white use cold, and the green dragonborn use poison.
 
They are resistant to elements based on their draconic ancestry, same as breath weapons.
 
During her work in the infirmary, Azlyn discovers ways to create ointments to clean wounds using Balsam, prepare potions of minor healing with Nettle, in addition to Lionsmane, Lavander and Rose. Azlyn is also warned about Belladonna, also known as Wolfsbane found in the region. Its appearance was similar to Balsam and if you were not careful, mixing the two could be the cause for instant death.
