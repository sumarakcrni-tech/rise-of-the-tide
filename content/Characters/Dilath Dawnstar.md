### Description

- Dilath Dawnstar is in her humanoid form a 25 year old [[human]] lady.
- She has black hair and brown eyes.
- She has smooth skin.
- She stands 175 cm tall.
- She has a round, beautiful face.
- She is dressed fashionably.
- Dilath made herself a lair in High Forest where she lived with her "family" of Drakenguard. She was completely obsessed by curing Brain Rot, so much that she committed atrocities in [[the High Forest]], kidnapping men and women and experimenting on them, the same way her brother showed her. But alas, she was unable to find the cure. Instead, she found a way to ease her symptoms by using purple crystals she aptly named [[Dilathite]].
- The players found her journal [[Dilath's Journal]].

### Traits

- She has Brain Rot.
- She is obsessed with creating new family members, seemingly compensating for past trauma.
- In her quest to be loved and accepted, she only becomes more evil and twisted.


### Relationship

- Mother to [[Mairgesh]].
- Sister to Daleth.
