### Description

- Liarel is a female high [[elf]] rogue and Fenran's sister.
- She was being controlled by **Erethas** and forced to relive the trauma of the[[Fall of Selren Thelmar]].
- She has steely eyes and long, black hair.
- She has rugged skin and fair complexion.
- She stands 185 cm tall and has a lean build.
- She has a square, slightly fanciable face.
- She has a warpaint on her right eye.
- The party freed her from Erethas and with their help, she freed her brother from Erethas' control.
- After the party rescues her and the other Shad'rai members, she decides to join the scouting division of [[Secomber]] and serves as a forward scout. She is a capable and dexterous fighter who can hold her own.
- She actively helps the party in getting rid of Erethas and **[[Mairgesh]]** and the threat they pose.

### Traits

- She discreetly worships [[Sune]].
- She is a kind and generous.
- She is very good at keeping secrets.
- She is always joking, even at inappropriate times.

### Relationships

- [[Fenran Kurdar]] is her brother.
