### Description

- Dimbos is a male dragonborn blacksmith.
- He has a spiked head and brown eyes.
- He has ridged red scales.
- He stands 180cm (5'10") tall and has a muscular build.
- He has a sharp, unremarkable face.
- He has a lisp.

### Traits

- He proudly worships [[Selûne]].
- He is a very good diplomat and always works towards resolution of conflict.
- He treats adversaries with respect.
- He wants to know every side of a story before expressing an opinion.

### Relationships

- He actively supports the party in their smithing needs.
- Reports to [[Xoyas Greenseeker]].
