### Description

 - Olympia is a female [[human]] scribe trying to translate the [[Arcana Obscura]] alongside [[Werola Thynid]].
- She has long, wavy, gray hair and brown eyes.
- She has brown skin.
- She stands 172cm tall and has a regular build.
- She has an oblong, attractive face.
- She is missing a part of her left ear.
- She actively helps the party with the information they need and has a trove of knowledge about the **[[Cult of the Dragon]]**.

### Traits

- She quietly worships [[Chauntea]].
- She is a hopeless romantic.

### Relationships

- Working with [[Werola Thynid]], [[Melbor Jahre]] in the library inside [[Secomber]].
- Fond of the party but rarely shows it.
