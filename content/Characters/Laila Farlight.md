### Description
- Laila Farlight was a female [[half-elf]] working in the Constabulary.
- She had wavy, black hair and brown eyes.
- She had silky silver skin.
- She stood 169cm and has a regular build.
- She had an edgy, incredibly common face.
- She had an abstract tattoo on her left hand.
- She was a double agent investigating the [[Cult of the Dragon]] for [[Zaltran Mistheart]], but was killed in battle by the party during the [[Fall of Hoghaven]].

### Traits
- Shady personality

### Relationships
- Worked for [[Zaltran Mistheart]] as a double agent
