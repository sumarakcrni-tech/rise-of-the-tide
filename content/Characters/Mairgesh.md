### Description

- Mairgesh is a dragonborn fighter.
- He has brownish skin.
- He stands 182cm tall and has a regular build.
- He has a rugged look on his face.
- He has a large scar on his chest.
- After the party meets with Mairgesh in a hovel outside Hoghaven, they are defeated and left to die.

### Traits

- He rarely talks, but when he does, it's with emotion.
- He has a deep, booming voice.

### Relationships

- Known to have worked with [[Henry Serpentwind]] and [[Rulf Redstream]].
- Son of [[Dilath Dawnstar]].

### Plot Hook

- After our party meets with Mairgesh in a hovel outside Hoghaven, they are defeated and left to die by the Cult of The Dragon members. Mairgesh, Henry and Rulf continue their plans westward towards Mordath Hold. They are able to defeat the guards posted there and take the keep as their own base of operations. Guided by the voice of Dilath, Mairgesh conducts an excavation underneath Mordath Hold to reach Dilath who is creating an army of Drakenguard, malformed creations of drakes and humans, fused in an ironclad armour.
