### Description

- Mairgesh is a dragonborn fighter.
- He has brownish skin.
- He stands 182cm tall and has a regular build.
- He has a rugged look on his face.
- He has a large scar on his chest.
- After the party meets with Mairgesh in a hovel outside Hoghaven, they are defeated and left to die.

### Traits

- He rarely talks, but when he does, it's with emotion.
- He has a deep, booming voice.

### Relationships

- Known to have worked with [[Henry Serpentwind]] and [[Rulf Redstream]].
- Son of [[Dilath Dawnstar]].
