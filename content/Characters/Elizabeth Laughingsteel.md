### Description

- Elizabeth Laughingsteel is a female [[human]] ranger.
- She has short, braided, brown hair and brown eyes.
- She has soft golden skin.
- She stands 145cm (4'9") tall and has a beefy build.
- She has a round, very beautiful face.
- She has a bone to pick with both Dilath and [[Mairgesh]], and with Dilath out of the way, she is laser focused on [[Mairgesh]].
- She recently scouted Mordath Hold and found evidence of the [[Mairgesh]] and his followers gathering there. An army is preparing to march on [[Secomber]].
- Her current whereabouts are unknown.

### Traits

- She quietly worships [[Chauntea]].
- She is very impatient.
- She is very talkative.
- She spends every morning training.
- She believes that all dragonborns are planning to destroy the world.
- She wears a mask, covering her truesight eyes.

### Relationships

- She is one of the board members of Iron Eater Compound, alongside [[Zaltran Mistheart]] and [[Bronir Fusehorn]].
- She executed [[Rulf Redstream]] during the [[Bell Incident]] in [[Secomber]].
