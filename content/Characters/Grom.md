### Patents

>Patent No. 00001 

Name: Storm catcher  
Purpose: lightning collection unit  
Description:
The role of the device is to be deployed outside during stormy weather and gather lightning energy. Requires a storage unit.  
The device uses three rods at the top to attract lightning. Upon impact, the lightning bolt is guided thru a series of wire coils that incrementally transform the lightning energy into a manageable format. At the bottom of the device, there is a slot in which a storage unit can be inserted. If the storage unit is missing, the collected energy is dissipated into the ground using its legs. If the storage unit is full, the same process is used to prevent damage to the device and storage device. The device is held in place by three legs. Each leg has a spike at the end so the device can be firmly placed on the ground.  
The device is cylindrical. Its top resembles a mushroom top with the tree lightning rods. Rods can be folded inwards for ease of transport. Legs can also be folded upwards and fit neatly under the mushroom top.  
Dimensions, folded: 1400x600x600  
Dimensions, deployed: 2000x600x600  
The slot for a storage device is circular with six symmetrically spaced cuts. dimensions: 300x300  
**20lbs in total, 10 lbs of wood, 5lbs of Iron, 5lbs copper wire**
 
>Patent No. 00002  

Name: Lightning in a bottle  
Purpose: lightning storage unit  
Description:
The primary role of the device is to store energy gathered by the "Storm catcher" device. After charging, the device can be stored and used in other devices that can use stored energy to perform their respective functions.  
The main body consists of two parts: the main and secondary coil. The main coil forms an endless loop thru which the lightning can travel essentially "trapping" it. This process is not perfect and in each cycle, a small portion of the energy is lost. The device will completely drain thru this in approximately one month. A portion of the energy can be redirected to the secondary coil that is connected to the connection port. This way the energy can be accessed incrementally avoiding overloading connected devices.  
The device is spherical, with a wooden handle at one end and a connector on the opposite side. The connector is a circular design with six symmetrically spaced plates that are used to connect to other devices.  
The slot is the standard 300x300 design.  
Dimensions: 300x300x300  
**5lbs in total, 3 lbs copper wire, 1 lbs wood, 1 lbs iron**  
 
>Patent No. 00003


Name: Lampadina  
Purpose: Illumination  
Description:
The primary role of the device is to produce light. Its light frame and "endless" power source make it a great replacement for torches.  
The device has a crank on one side that is used to compress a series of springs that store kinetic energy and enable prolonged uses. As the springs unwind, they spin a metal core inside of a coil. This produces many sparks that light up the nearby area. The number of sparks makes the light seam a continuous light instead of many small flashes. WARNING: some creatures experience dizziness when the light is shined directly into their eyes.  
30 seconds of cranking can produce up to 30 minutes of light.  
The device is cylindrical with a glass window on top and a small crank on one side.  
dimensions: 300x300x300  
15 feet dim light, 10 bright light  
**5lbs in total, 3 lbs copper wire, 1 lbs wood, 1 lbs iron**
