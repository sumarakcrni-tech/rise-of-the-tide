### Description

Karvem Shog is an older male [[human]] warrior.

- He is bald with brown eyes with a full black beard.
- He has soft brown skin.
- He stands 177cm (5' 8") tall and has an athletic build.
- He has an oval face.
- He has slightly long fingers.
- He was rescued by the members of the Order of Bahamut whom he know openly worships. Can understand Thieves' Cant.
- After being unable to find the Arcana Obscura, he loses morale and has an identity crisis about who he wants to serve. Meeting our adventurers however, gives him a new purpose. And by small chance, he recognizes the red-haired lady that was kicked out of house Wyndall. At first, he isn't sure, but after talking to her, his doubts fade away. Even though she is no longer part of house Wyndall, Karvem still wants to protect her. 
- He flees [[Hoghaven]] after the [[Fall of Hoghaven]] together with the remaining villagers. In the meantime, Karvem explains that Lord Wyndall had a plan to turn [[Hoghaven]] into a place where the construction of a flying ship would happen since the village is close to High Forest. He also tells Talia that her brother, Theodor Wyndall, is looking for her, but he doesn't know why.

### Traits

- He openly worships Bahamut.
- He is very direct.
- He is very courageous, to a fault.


### Relationships

- Personal guard of the Wyndall family, including [[Ren Wyndall]], [[Talia Wyndall]].
- He is close with the people in charge of Iron Eater Compound and actively seeks to help them out in everyday work.
- He also revealed that he used to be a member of the Order of Bahamut, dedicated to preventing the dragon threat of destroying the world.
