### Description

- Celorfin Garaitinu is a male high [[elf]] blacksmith.
- He has long, wavy, brown and blue eyes.
- He has soft brown skin.
- He stands 147cm (4'9") tall and has an athletic build.
- He has an oval, incredibly glamorous face.
- He is branded as a slave on his chest.
- He has slightly long fingers.
- He was one of the survivors of the [[Fall of Hoghaven]] and escaped with the party to [[Secomber]] where he now works as a blacksmith, aiding the party.

### Traits

- He openly worships [[Mystra]].
- He is very direct.
- He is very courageous, to a fault.

### Relationships

- Married.
- Fond of [[Coins]] who is his apprentice.
- Fond of [[Grom]].
- Celorfin and [[Coins]] are escaped slaves of [[The Flaming Rose Company]] that settled in [[Hoghaven]] as blacksmiths while the village was still unknown to most people. But as the villages popularity rose, so did their suspicion in other people.
- Celorfin is currently building Patent No. 4 on behest of [[Grom]].
