### Description

- **Bronir** is a male [[human]] soldier.
- He has brown eyes and brown hair.
- He has smooth, pinkish skin.
- He stands 177 cm tall and has a lean build.
- He has an oval face with a neatly trimmed beard.
- He actively helps the party in their efforts and trusts them wholeheartedly.
- He used to be an excellent drill sergeant, but has not shown himself as a capable politician or a leader recently.
- He is currently missing from [[Secomber]].

### Traits

- He proudly worships [[Silvanus]].
- He has a highly variable mood.
- He is an example of modesty.
- He intermittently quotes proverbs.

### Relationships

- Married.
- He is one of the board members of Iron Eater Compound, alongside [[Elizabeth Laughingsteel]] and [[Zaltran Mistheart]].
