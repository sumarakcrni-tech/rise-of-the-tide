Our adventure continues with our party members in The Slush, discussing their next move. Grom proposes an investigation into the graffitied houses in the Slush that were mentioned by Bronir. As they make their way through the district, they spot a single house graffitied with a symbol resembling the Cult of The Dragon. They observe the house from the outside, staring through the window to see if there is someone inside. Talia decides to knock on the door and a man comes by the window from the inside. He asks her what do they want, and she replies that they wish to talk about the graffiti. The man nods and opens the door. He introduces himself as Zeirad, a journalist for the Iron Hawk and he is certain that Henry Serpentwind and his cronies are behind the outrageous graffiti. The party wonders how is he certain that they did it, and he starts going off and never stopping. Talia, frustrated by his never-ending story, interrupts him and tells him to get to the point. Zeirad tells them that he is certain that Henry and his cronies are behind it since he saw Henry's butcher flying towards his house. The party thinks this is absurd and questions Zeirad to which he responds that it's true, that they even got into his house and were trying to find the article he was writing about The Cult of The Dragon to impress Olympia. Grom is unamused by his half-truths and wonders if Zeirad remembers where he put the article to which he responds that it was put into a book called "Article 5". They wonder how does he know so much about the Cult, and he responds by saying that his employer, Zaltran, is the one who has all the info. The party thank him and leave him; they were planning on visiting Zaltran anyway. As they make their way out of the Slush, Talia hears a strange sound coming from her backpack, as if someone was knocking against the glass. She searches through her backpack and finds the sound coming from the gems that were given to them by Marvus. Two Netherese humans trapped in sapphires, an imp trapped in the ruby, and a strange, reptilian creature trapped in the emerald. The party is slightly disturbed by this, but they press on to Zaltran's house. As they arrive to the High Road, the party presses on towards the house near the lake. Zaltran welcomes them in. He wonders if they found the astrolabe and Grom shows it to him. He observes it meticolously, mumbling about patterns and stars and he concludes that it will do nicely. He offers them 200 gold, evenly split amongs the party, but only as a joke. He then gives them the promised 800 gold and the party splits it evenly. He gives them a quick history on the Cult, and mentions that he has every right to believe that Severin Silrajin is currently planning something for the Cult in the Western Heartlands. Zaltran briefly mentions the Wyrmskull Throne which was said to be able to control the blue dragons. They talk to him about the graffiti and how they met with Zeirad to which he responds that Zeirad is always embelishing things, but he is concerned about Henry and his cronies. The party wonders how he knows about him, to which he replies that he had an agent infiltrate Hoghaven, but sadly, she hasn't reported her findings in the last five days. Grom and Talia look at each other with surprise and they mention that his agent was an unfortunate casualty during their scuffle with Mairgesh, Henry and Rulf the Butcher. Zaltran, visibly annoyed, tells them to leave him be and to let him think. After a couple of minutes, he comes out of his house and decides that they need to launch an assault on Hoghaven. Grom wonders why, since he believes that they left Hoghaven and that they could be already in Secomber, waiting for a perfect time to strike. Zaltran is confused at first, but starts to understand when Grom explains what had happened in Hoghaven. As they all speak outside his house, the bells start to toll. The party is combat ready, but the bells continue tolling, without rest. Zaltran, hurriedly tells them that they have to go to Compound. They rush towards the Compound to befuddled guards who are also wondering why the bells are tolling. Fortunately, there was no attack. They make their way into the Compound and go to Bronir to see what in the seven hells is going on. Bronir assures them that everything is probably fine and there was most likely a malfunction on one of the bells. Zaltran is not amused by Bronir and threatens to give him a spanking, to which Bronir just looks at him confused. The bells finally stop after a couple of minutes. The party is finding all of this suspicious and decides to investigate it further, to which Bronir agrees. The party wonders if there is a ledger of everyone going in and out of Secomber, to which Bronir nods. He tells Zaltran to go and find the ledger of all visitors who came into the city in case someone slipped in unnoticed. An angry Zaltran walks out of the office. Before the party leaves the office, Karvem stops Talia and tells her that her brother, Ren Wyndall, will be visiting Secomber in a couple of days. He also tells her that it seems her other brother is missing. She wonders how does he know all of this, to which he replies that he read between the lines in the last message that was sent to Bronir from the Lord of Secomber. He tells her to be safe, to which she nods. She joins the rest of the party in the hallways of Iron Eater Compound before moving out to investigate the bells.
