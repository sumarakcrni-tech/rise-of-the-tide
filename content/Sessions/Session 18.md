Our adventurers continue their plans by meeting with Zaltran in the library. Zaltran, still preoccupied with searching for the ledger, brushes them away. Talia asks Olympia if she knows the ledger's location to which she points her to the corner of the library. Talia finds the ledger and tries to understand what is written on it, but it's mostly gibberish. The party notices that some of the dates are not written down on page 3 for a bunch of caravans that passed through Secomber, moving southbound. Talia questions Olympia who is responsible for updating the ledger and she points to the man behind them. Melbor Jahre. Talia tries to ask him, but he does not respond. Olympia tells them that he cannot hear. Talia grabs a piece of paper and starts writing questions. Melbor observes them curiously and face-palms them after reading one of the questions. He points to the ledger and makes a gesture to give it to him. He populates it and Talia reads the Mirtul 3 and Mirtul 4 as the dates when the caravans passed. Talia thanks him and goes to deliver the ledger to Zaltran. Zaltran, startled by Talia, thanks her for the help, even if he did not need it. Grom persuades Zaltran to come with them to discuss a matter of great importance. Zaltran obliges, reluctantly.
 
They move into the living quarters inside Iron Eater Compound, making sure nobody is following them. Zaltran asks what the fuss is about, and Talia takes out a couple of gemstones with people inside them. She asks Zaltran if he knows anything about that. Zaltran, completely stunned by what he had seen, tells them that it is possibly a Minimus Containment spell. He recalls that the way to leave the gemstone is by finishing a task given to the creatures by the caster of the spell. Talia tries communicating with the people inside the gemstone, but they are not in a cooperative mood. She asks Zaltran what would happen to the ones inside the gemstone if it was used in a ritual, and Zaltran responds saying that he does not know. Suddenly, a pressure builds up in the room, trying to pull them down into the ground of the room they are in. A flaming sphere appears out of nowhere and a tiefling walks out of it. Marvus. He grabs a ruby from his pocket, containing a devil and hands it over to Talia, saying: "You dropped one." Talia asks him why he is giving her these gemstones, and he responds by saying: "Why not?" She tries to get some info out of him about why they are inside the gemstones, and he responds by simply saying: "They did this to themselves. A reminder of greed." He takes his leave through the flaming sphere and vanishes into thin air. Zaltran finally understands what they were talking about and is completely befuddled.
 
After more discussions with Zaltran, he requests the party to visit the Bell Tower and see what is happening there. His gut feeling tells him that something is wrong with it. He promises the party to give them 40 gold in total and the Book of The Dragon he holds in possession. The party, reluctantly, agrees. They leave the Iron Eater Compound and head south, towards the Bell Tower. After a long trudge through Secomber, the party finally arrives at the location. They find two guards posted in front of it. After explaining to the guards that they were sent by Bronir to investigate, the guards oblige and answer all questions the party has. However, Grom has a hunch that the guards may be hiding something. It seems that they were sleeping on duty. Grom gives them tips about how to do it effectively, and promises not to tell on them, in exchange that they tell the truth. The guards oblige and say that the Lord's Guard is at the top of the Bell Tower, currently working on repairs. The adventurers decide to pay them a visit. As they ascend the tower, Luxon starts feeling uneasy by the whole ordeal. They arrive at the apex of the bell tower and notice two behemoths in iron clad armour, repairing the broken bell clanger. The party introduces themselves and the muffled voices respond in kind. The party investigates and tries to figure out what had happened, but the muffled voices from the helmet offer no immediate answers. A sense of dread washes over Luxon. He rushes outside the Tower, trying to recollect his thoughts. Grom, rather annoyed by the muffled behemoths, tries to take a more "hands-on" approach with one of the guards. Grom politely asks the guard to remove his helmet so they can understand him better, but the guard refuses. Luxon goes back into the Tower and pulls Talia outside, telling her that one of the muffled voices is the butcher from Hoghaven, he's sure of it.
 
Grom, ever so handy in his approach, tries to push the guard to knock his helmet off, but fails. The guard takes of his helmet and is recognized as Henry Serpentwind. He swings his sword at Grom, damaging his armour. Luxon and Talia rejoin them at the summit, only to see the behemoths fighting Grom and Azlyn. They intervene and take down the second, axe-wielding behemoth. Azlyn magically binds Henry to a stand still, allowing Grom to deliver a stunning blow to his head, knocking Henry prone. Luxon descends the tower to get help from the guards, who with a little help from Talia, persuades them to join him. They ascend the tower, only to see the axe-wielding behemoth laying prone and reaching for his axe. Azlyn knocks the axe away from him, but the behemoth regains his composure and grabs it, lunging towards Talia. Azlyn slashes the behemoth with a mighty blow from her greatsword, causing him to stumble back and right into a guardsmen spear. Henry and Grom continue battling on the side, with Henry trying to slash Grom's armour, but to no avail. Grom charges up his fist and punches Henry straight in the head, causing him to fly outside the opening and splatter across the floor below the bell tower. This angers the second behemoth who charges at Talia once more, slashing her open with his axe. But he is no match to the combined might of our party. They take him down, piece by piece, and tie him down. Grom takes the helmet off him, and reveals Rulf Redstream, the butcher of Hoghaven.
 
The party descends the tower with their new prisoner. They stop by Henry's mangled corpse and notice that his armor is still emitting a strange odorless smoke. They observe his blade which has draconic runes on him which reveal themselves to Luxon, spelling out "Mairgesh." The party tries to take the sword away from him, but is unable to. They give up on the sword and leave with their captive, back to Iron Eater Compound.
