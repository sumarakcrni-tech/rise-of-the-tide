As our adventurers leave the Iron Eater Compound, they form a consensus about visiting Shorty's Grove to solve the fog problem inside the cave. They make their way towards the site in a brisk pace from the Compound and they meet up with the guards posted outside the cave. They speak to the guards and explain that Bronir sent them to deal with the problem, and the guards nod in agreement. Both guards move towards the two-winch mechanism which opens the gates of the mine. As the gates open, a thick fog surrounds the premises. The guards warn them to be careful, but our adventurers are already walking inside. Slowly but surely, they make their way through the thick fog inside the mine, following the rail tracks leading them deeper into the mine. They make their way towards the cart filled with coal and they notice that the fog is getting thicker. They search around the cart to but they don't find anything of worth. Finally, Grom decides to go through the coal in the cart and finds a strange mechanical device with two cranks on each side of the device. He presses on one of them, and the fog stops emitting. He observes the machinery closely and notices some dwarven runes inscribed on it, spelling out Kori. Meanwhile, Talia remembers that the Otyugh they rescued from here told them about how the murder that happened here. Grom decides to pull the cart towards himself, and as he does that, they notice a dark red, turning to black stain on the ground. Luxon hears a strange voice in his head, speaking about Harmand and wondering why is he doing this. The voice fades. They conclude that this must be the murder scene where Noryver Barrelchin was killed. Unable to find anything else, they leave the mine. The guards there are wondering how did they get rid of the fog and the party just says "Tools of the trade." They ask the guards if they remember anything about an accident that happenned in the mine, and the guards respond by saying that there is a work ledger for all workers in the mine. They did hear a story from one of the other guards when the incident happened, how they heard a large thud and when they rushed in, they saw a dwarf crying over the body of his brother. The dwarfs name in question was Harmand. The guards tell them that Harmand left Secomber not long after the incident happened. The other guards does mention that he remembers an incident with Harmand and how he got kicked out of a tavern after he had a couple of drinks, spouting how he was jealous of his brother for marrying Tharkar. The party finds all of this interesting information, but then the bells start to toll. Five tolls. One of the guards runs up to them, completely out of breath and yells at the top of his lungs, "Fire! In the Slush Docks! Quickly!" The party rushes towards the docks, and they are met with a fiery scene of two ships completely on fire. The guards are rushing past them with buckets of water, but to no avail. The party wades through the smoke-filled docks to get a closer look at the ships with surprising fortitude. Grom takes out the machine they found in the mine and decides to turn it on. The machine starts letting out a fog going upwards in a 90 degrees angle. He notices the other crank and starts cranking on it. As he cranks, the angle of the fog lowers and he points it at the fire. The fog absorbs the fire and they manage to stop the fire from spreading. But the ships are left only as blackened, charred husks of what they once were. As they start moving closer towards one of the ships, they hear footsteps coming from inside the hull of the ship. Grom grabs the hull of the ship and tries to lower it so he can see what is going on, and in the meantime, Talia decides to climb on Grom's shoulders which he finds unamusing. As Talia peers over the edge of the ship, a small winged creature flies out the lower decks of the ship. A devilish imp. Talia climbs onto the ship and takes a closer look at him. The imp flies towards her and yells, "Trinkets?!" Talia says that she doesn't have any and the imp responds, "Buy trinkets?" The footsteps from the lower decks are heard again. "Oh no, the master comes," the imp speaks out loud and a large figure appears from the lower decks. A tiefling dressed in a red suit with a tophat. The imp flies away in fear. Talia asks the tiefling who he might be and he responds by saying, "Marvus. How can I help you, do you need a deal?" Talia asks him if he is okay and why didn't he notice the fire that was raging a couple of seconds ago to which he ignores the question. Grom tries to get some answers from him, but his mind starts suggesting him to keep his mouth shut. Marvus explains to Talia that he offers deals and information, but only if they sign a contract. Talia asks him if he knows something about Harmand and Tharkar and he mentions that Tharkar and Marvus have a deal in place, grinning wildly. Marvus offers them some free information, but Talia declines at first. He snaps his fingers at her and tells her to come over. Talia's body fights against it, but is ultimately unable to disobey him. He tells her that the next time someone offers something for free, the better option would be to take it. Talia nods. He whistles and the imp comes back. He tells him to give them trinkets, 2 sapphire, 1 ruby and 1 malachite. The imp frightingly searches his bag and tosses them over to Talia. Marvus tells them to get off the ship and leave him immediately. The party nods and leaves the premises of the docks and goes back towards the Slush. One of the guards stops them and tells them to talk with the recquisitions manager near the docks that is responsible for tracking everyone. They agree and talk to the recquisitions officer about what had happened on the docks and he confirms that he was also unaware what had happened. He saw a dwarf in a black coat leaving the docks early in the morning with one of their fishing vessels that can be rented out, but he was nowhere to be found after the fire happened. He also confirms that the ships that were in the fires belong to the Barrelchin family. The party questions the officer about what any additional info, but the officers shrugs, signaling that is the only info they have for now. The party thank the officer and leave his office, planning their next move.
