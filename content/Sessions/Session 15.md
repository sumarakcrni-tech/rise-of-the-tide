As our adventurers successfully remove the mind controlling jaws of their adversaries, the remaining forces of Selren Themar come to their senses. They try to interrogate the elven survivors, but they are mostly unable to answer their questions. The elves speak about Selren Themar being an outpost that was supposed to scout Orogoth and eliminate a dracolich residing there. They remember a red shadow appearing over their settlement and starting to burn everything to the ground. They remember Erethas trying to ressurect a dracolich in Orogoth, but they got captured during the scouting mission. They were tortured and finally mind-controlled. They mention there could be one of their remaining settlements in the Misty Forest, but our adventurers propose a different idea. They want the elves to join them in Secomber. After some time, the elves agree and the party travels to Secomber and straight towards the Iron Eater Compound. Grom and Talia tell the guards to go grab Bronir and that they have a proposal for him. The guard comes back with Bronir who seems keen at the idea of the elves staying there. They agree to let the elves stay, but they must adhere to the same rules as everyone else at the Compound. The party rests for the day after a grueling trip to the swamp. Grom decides to get up earlier and start work on his patents. Celorfin notices him and asks to help, to which Grom accepts. After a couple of hours, the rest of the party wakes up and goes about their day. Luxon visits the library to finally start working and earn some money. He is given a book to translate that is written in Abyssal. He understand some parts of it, which are connected to something called the Plane of Pleasure. Meanwhile, Azlyn visits the infirmary in hope to get some bandages and water to clean herself up. She meets up with Mesarth who promises to get her what she needs in a couple of minutes. She agrees to his help. Talia visits the smithy and notices Grom with Celorfin huddled over a table. She sneaks up on Grom and starts asking him about the design they talked about. Grom is dismissive at first, but Celorfin agrees to help with the construction of it. The party meet up and visit Bronir who is talking to Karvem. Karvem informs them that Talia's brother is visiting Secomber in a couple of days with the Lord of Secomber. Talia is annoyed at this prospect, but also intrigued by Karvem's ability to read between the lines. Bronir thanks them for notifying him, and thanks the party for what they did in Shorty's Grove. But there is another matter at hand. The fog appeared in the cave again. Bronir also tells them to report to the library before heading out. Our adventurers visit the library and meet up with Olympia and Werola with the astrolabe they found. Werola takes the astrolabe and puts it into a socket in the machine. The machine starts working and the room starts turning blue. Luxon decides to take a few steps back during this violent outburst. Werola gives the Arcana Obscura to Talia who then starts going through the pages until it starts floating away from her hands and starts levitating. Two blue gauges appear and Talia starts turning them. The machine starts slowing down and several images flash by. An image of a great purple tree with a blue canopy called the Wayspire, located in Arcadia. Another image appears and a giant white spire appears before them. Talia tries turning the gauges, but nothing else appears. Werola takes out the astrolabe from the machine and it powers down. Werola and the party concur that the machine is probably some kind of a device that teleports the user to other dimensions. The party observes the machine and notices some other missing parts and inputs, particularly an S shaped hole with a lightning symbol on it. Grom notices several cogs that are missing from the machine and notes that down. Olympia starts talking out loud and wonders how did Zaltran know about this and did he know this was going to happen. The party wonders the same and decides to pay him a visit. But, there are other things still looming above them. Where will they go next?
