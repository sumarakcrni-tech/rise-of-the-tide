Secomber was a small, peaceful town that acted as the de facto border town between the relatively peaceful Western Heartlands and the more savage North along the Sword Coast. The town contained a number of colorful gardens. Secomber was built on the western ruins of Hastarl, capital of the ancient wizard kingdom of Athalantar.
 
It sat atop three stone hills on the northern banks of the southern-running Unicorn Run river and the western-running River Delimbiyr. Directly south of the town was the dangerous High Moor. To the north, along the Secomber Trail, was [Hoghaven](My%20Notebook/Settlements/Hoghaven.md).
 
Farming, fishing, and stone-cutting were the primary occupations, but it also had a number of folk familiar with the [High Forest](The%20High%20Forest.md). Some locals hire themselves out as guides or guards for the passing caravans.
 
A small garrison of about 30, provided by the Lords' Alliance, guarded the town, and the local militia boasted over 100 members.

# **First Encounter**

1. # **First Encounter**
    

As our adventurers were gravely wounded from their encounter with [[Mairgesh]], a chosen of Tiamat, the residents of [[Hoghaven]] they rescued bring them into Secomber, Iron Eater Compound to be specific. As they slowly regain their composure, [[Grom]] is awakened in a blacksmith shop with new vigor, and a pair of flamethrower gauntlets. On the other side of the Compound, a weary Talia and Azlyn rise from their slumber in an infirmary. Talia sees Kharvem and for the first time in a long time, they speak openly. Kharvem says that he recognizes her from back when she was a small child in Waterdeep, as part of Lord Wyndall's house. They speak about Theodor and Ren, Talia's two brothers and what are they doing. Talia mentions a note saying that Theodor left her a message in [[Hoghaven]] in Thieves' Cant. Meanwhile, [[Luxon]] wakes up and overhears them speaking. Meanwhile, [[Grom]] decides to visit the local library with Celorfin and finds Werola there. They catch up and also speak to the owner of the Library, [Olympia Skyriver](Olympia%20Skyriver), who charges a hefty price to read any book. In the meantime, Azlyn, Talia, [[Mesarth]], Kharvem and [[Luxon]] leave the infirmary and start going to the library, but before that, they spot a plaque written in Draconic. [[Mesarth]] deciphers it and explains that the text speaks of the Defenders of Bahamut and how this place was one of their bastions. They enter the library and catch up with Werola, but not before Azlyn and Olympia get in a heated argument. Talia shows that she has the _Arcana Obscura_ book, and when she starts opening the pages, a strange machine behind Werola activates. Olympia explains that the device was a gift from Lord Wyndall that he got from an expedition down South. She says that one can navigate using this machine, but she had no idea how to operate it. It seems that the _Arcana Obscura_ operates the machine. They start to leave the library, but not before [[Grom]] helps Olympia decipher a dwarven book containing blueprints for a cannon and a hand cannon. As they leave the library and head into the living quarters, our weary adventurers are finally feeling safe. Talia and Kharvem come clean about Lord Wyndall and how she is his daughter, Kharvem recalls his childhood back when he was a young kid in Icewind Dale and how a group of guardians saved him from [The Cult](Cult%20of%20the%20Dragon.md), they speak of the [[Netherese]] and [[Mesarth]] mentions the Red Rose, showing the slave mark he has on his back. They decide to rest here and take a break from everything that had been going on.
 
Another thing to mention is that Talia read a book that depicted a shadowy-boar creature, but it does not seem relevant. Yet.
