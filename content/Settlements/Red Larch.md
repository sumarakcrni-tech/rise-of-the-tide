The town of **Red Larch** was a waystop on the Long Road seven days north of Waterdeep, and was located at the intersection of three trails. One trail led to the Bargewright Inn, another to Kheldell, and the third ran into the hills to derelict, monster-infested keeps. The town was named for a stand of red larch trees that were chopped down about the time of the town's founding.

# **Plot Points**

The town of Red Larch has been a victim of a terrible betrayal by the hands of Fendrel Mistsplitter.  
When a small, flying creature appeared from the Feywild, the men of Red Larch started disappearing. The only two remaining men were Fendrel and Alfred.
 
On the road to Red Larch, a group of bandits attacked a trading caravan bound for Triboar. What they found inside would soon be their downfall. Our adventureres were stopped by the bandits, which was their biggest mistake. The bandits fell by the hands of our adventureres, who soon found out what had happenned to their leader. Spiral tattoos appeared over his body, a strange amulet around his neck.
 
On the outskirts of the town, a band of outcasts attacked and siezed the Barracks of Red Larch. They terrorized the local populace until a band of adventureres put a stop to them. But little did they know, the bandits unearthed something far more sinister. A phaerimm was laying dormant underneath the ruined manor near Red Larch. They encountered the small flying creature from the Feywild and escaped the Phaerimm with it.
