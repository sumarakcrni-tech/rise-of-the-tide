# **First Encounter**

1.  **First Encounter**
    *The forest provides shelter.*

As our adventurers leave Hoghaven with the resources they scavanged, Luxon starts hearing voices in his head, calling him for help, saying to go Eastern Cave. Luckily, the party spots the cave on the local map they found in the Constabulary. After a couple of minutes bickering, Luxon and Talia decide that the party should stop there, just in case.
 
They stop at the cave entrance and hear voices coming from the inside. Luxon and Talia manage to evade the carefully laid out trap and observe the shattered pieces of a large statue. Meanwhile, Azlyn tries to follow them, but triggers the trap, causing a bunch of rocks to fall on her, knocking her unconscious. Grom, already frustrated with Azlyn's inability, stabilizes her and throws her on the cart.
 
Talia and Luxon proceed deeper into the cave, hearing a voice calling for help. But before they can get there, Luxon spots another trap that Talia disarms with her Mage Hand. They rush towards the wounded wood elf and call for Mesarth to provide the necessary aid. He flusters around but Talia calms him. He heals the wounded wood elf who falls unconscious. They grab the wounded elf and her dead partner and place them on the cart and continue towards their campsite.
 
Back at the campsite, Werola and Cedric prepare a campfire and store the food they scavanged. But suddenly, a blue light appears from the ground. The party arrives at the campsite, but ignores the light. They start cutting wood to build a simple shelter for the night.
 
But then, they start hearing footsteps from the [High Forest](The%20High%20Forest.md). Eyes glowing in the dark, between the trees. The wooden footsteps draw ever closer, and the party prepares for a fight. But suddenly, the footsteps stop and retreat back into the woods. Talia determines it is possible that those were Wood Woads. Grom noticed something strange on one of the trees. A spiral marking.
 
As the night continues, the sounds can be heard again coming from the forest, but never come closer. The wood elf called Wynra woke up. She thanked them for rescuing her and spoke about how she and Lyza fled a shadowy creature into the cave where we found them. By some strange coincidence, or perhaps fate, the campsite our party went to was near one of wood elves settlements, in a cave below ground level. The party asked about those strange spiral markings on the trees, and Wynra mentioned [[the Flaming Rose Company]] and how they are the ones doing that. To what end, nobody knows.
 
Wynra asks the party if they are willing to join her in venturing in the cave underground, but Grom and Azlyn say no. Talia and Luxon, on the other hand, accept her proposal and venture with her underground.
