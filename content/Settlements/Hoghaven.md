Nestled underneath the [High Forest](The%20High%20Forest.md), a trading village of Hoghaven resides. The village gained in popularity in recent years with its export of hogs, spices and lumber. This piqued the interest of lords in Waterdeep who wish to serve their own interest. One of those lords was Lord Wyndall, Talia's father.
 
# **Plot Points**

The village of Hoghaven is a crossroads for all kinds of people. Their recent fame has left the conservative villagers suspicious of everyone and everything. A group of would-be adventurers arrived at the doorstep of Hoghaven recently.
 
1. **The Beginning**

The intelligent machine known only as Grom met with a cleric woman called Azlyn Benett near a library where a robbery happened. The guards informed them to speak to Werola, the local librarian. Werola explained to Grom that pages were torn from the books which was odd, and another book called _Arcana Obscura_ was stolen which seemed to really upset Werola. Grom and Azlyn promised to help her find the book that was stolen, for a price, of course. Werola agreed and pointed them east bound.
 
In front of the constabulary, they met a strange man called Luxon who had just arrived to town. Grom and Azlyn did not interact much with him, but they had the same idea. They proceeded to visit the Constabulary ran by Leo Cartwright to obtain more information about the recent attack. He provided little information, saying only that bandits were spotted on the Eastern Road. He did however, offer a bounty prize for the bandits. Grom, Azlyn and Luxon accepted it, which led to bickering which was resolved by agreeing to split the bounty between them. Grom wandered around the constabulary and silently observed Leo's assistant, Laila. She told him to get back and leave her alone, to which Grom obliged, but not before noticing that she had a strange spiral tattoo on her left hand and some strange papers she was looking at.
 
Leo remembered there had been a strange occurrence in the village, where a hog from the Cleves Family Farm had been stolen, which the former blamed on his rival family, The Hardcloaks. They agreed to investigate. The first place to start would be the local tavern.
 
On their way to the tavern, Azlyn wished to talk to the Cleves Family members, but was swiftly rejected. She did notice that there were no windows on the little hovel the family was situated. Grom decided to stop by the local church where he encountered a dragonkin acolyte called Mesarth. They chatted about the missing book and Werola, but he did not hear anything. He did say that what happened was awful and he had hoped if Grom could retrieve the stolen books so Mesarth could organize a book reading competition. Grom reluctantly agreed. With Azlyn and Luxon already gone, Grom continued on towards the tavern, but sidetracked when he spotted a sign saying _Smithy_.
 
The machine entered the shop and met Celorfin, an elven blacksmith with his Kenku apprentice, Coins. They chatted about the recent bandit attacks, but he did not hear anything. Grom asked for some help in his blacksmithing endevours and Celorfin obliged. After a couple of tips and tricks, Grom thanked the man and left the smithy for the tavern.
 
1. **The Tavern**

Inside the tavern they met a strange elven woman that was performing in front a crowd of guardsmen. The crowd cheered on for her as she continued to enchant them with a tamburine/drum combo that left the crowd roaring for more. No lasting effort on our adventurers, though. Grom, Azlyn and Luxon sat at an empty table and the elven woman joined them. She introduced herself as Talia Wyndall. They chatted about the missing book that Werola mentioned and the reward for returning it, which prompted Talia to ask to join them. After a round of drinks, they proceeded into the village to explore.
 
When they got out of the tavern, Luxon noticed something strange. A voice spoke to him inside his head. Mentioning the Eastern Road. He noticed a small particle of dried-up blood on the floor near the tavern, and deducted it was the blood of a dragonkin. This did seem rather strange to everyone around him. He ignored them and they proceeded together to to square where a circus had set up shop.
 
They talked to the merchants who offered them their wares, when they were interrupted by a dwarven man called Gili Hardcloak. He spoke how Cedric blames his family for the missing hog, which was absurd nonsense and asks the adventurers to help clear his name. They agree to investigate, but not before Talia notices that he has a strange tattoo on his right hand. They investigated the Cleves Farm for any signs of trespass, but found none. Talia caught eye of a strange cart sitting outside the Library, and decided to take a sneak peek into the cart. Azlyn followed. They found three crates shut tight and tried to open them, but to no avail. One of the guards from the Library came out to guard it, but they slipped past him, their cover intact.
 
Luxon, on the other hand, investigated the pig pen of the Hardcloak family when he heard voices calling to him. Somebody, or something, was fed to the pigs of the Hardcloak family. The news were disturbing.
 
1. **The Travel**

The adventurers continued talking to the merchants, when a ring caught Talia's eye. Being the thief that she is, she pocketed it, creating a minor illusion in its place. The merchant never noticed the difference, and when he did, they were already long gone. Their decision was to proceed into the Eastern Road. After a couple of hours, the adventurers returned, battered and bruised and rested in the inn.
 
1. **T****he Mystery Deepens**

Azlyn and Talia woke up at night. Something was dripping from one of the cupboards. Blood. They called for Grom and Luxon who investigated what was inside the cupboard. A head of a dead pig. The news was troubling. The head had been magically clotted to stop it from bleeding until a certain time.
 
They moved out the tavern and into the dimly lit roads of Hoghaven. Talia spotted a guard near the cart next to the Library which seemed odd. The team planned an elaborate heist from the Constabulary to steal the strange papers Grom noticed.
 
Talia went scouting and found a place of entry to the constabulary. The back window. She noticed a single guard awake next to an elven prisoner. The plan quickly fell into chaos as Talia slipped while entering through the window. The guard did not spot her, but went to wake up the other guards. Talia quickly went in and lockpicked the gate, allowing the elf to open the portcullis and quickly escape. She soon realized that it was Cedric Cleves who she had rescued. They regrouped with the rest of the team, going with him into his house.
 
1. **The Hogs of Hoghaven**

Cedric spoke about his missing hog, to which Talia created an illusion of the hog head that was found in the inn. Cedric fell into tears and anger as he saw his beloved hog, now confirmed dead and slain. They spoke about how Laila wants to overthrow Leo from the Constabulary, how Hardcloaks brand their pigs on the tongue and how much he hates the aristocracy. He gave them a bunch of strange notes that appearred to be transactional data with the timeline when a caravan appeared in the city, about a month ago. He did not know why his hog was branded by the Hardcloaks only to be slaughtered.
 
Grom suggested to escort Cedric out of the city, since he was a wanted man. Cedric said that he wishes to visit Werola as she is the only person he can trust. Azlyn and Talia offered to disguise him before they went to the library to avoid drawing attention. They went out into the night, slowly creeping towards the library, avoiding the guards. Werola was half asleep when they knocked on her door. She opened them because of Grom. They explained the whole situation to her and she decided to help them hide Cedric in the Library. She gave our adventurers a map of Faerun and hid underneath the giant table with Cedric. Cedric informed them that Laila resides in the inn, the first room on the right.
 
Our party recalled the guard that was guarding the cart near the library, and they really wanted to know what he was hiding. They devised a plan which included creating an iron ring that would be placed on the guard using a mage hand, and Grom would then shock the guard, sending him into a paralysis. The plan worked flawlessly. The guard collapsed and they went to loot the crates. They found 4 strange robes with a wooden mask, 4 silver bracelets and a ruby eye bracelet. Underneath that, a small wooden necklace lay. Azlyn recognized it as one of the Leshen's gifts. In the final crate, they found a **mysterious egg**, warm to the touch.
 
The party proceeded to the inn where they were greeted by the Innkeeper, Henry Serpentwind. They went to Talia's room, but suddenly, they got locked in. They tried every way to get out, until Grom decided enough was enough. He stormed out of the room, shattering the door in the process. He was greeted by two guards, mistaking him for Leo Cartwright. Grom persuaded them to go downstairs with him and apologize to the Innkeeper, and to pay for repairs. Grom explained the situation to the Innkeeper, and soon learned that the previous owner of the room was Leo Cartwright. The pieces were falling into place.
 
Meanwhile, up on the second floor, Talia decided to do a little sneak peek into Laila's room. She picked her lock, only to be greeted by Laila, who closed the door and locked it again. Talia and Luxon then decided to see if there was a way to spy on her from the room next door. Talia went up on the roof and observed the room through the window. She created a distraction with a foul smell which forced Laila to get out of the room. Talia then swiftly entered the room and rummaged through Laila's possessions, finding a note with 4 names, Laila, Rulf, Henry and Hardcloak, also, a note speaking of meeting with someone West.
 
Grom went upstairs and met up with the rest of the party, when they decided to get out. Luxon went down the stairs first and nearly perished. The warm and welcoming mask of the Innkeeper fell as he opened the back door where a mountain of man rushed towards the guards that were still down there and planted his meat cleaver between his eyes.  
Luxon ejected himself from the window, running away from the Butcher Rulf.
 
Azlyn, Grom and Talia decided to leave the inn ASAP, jumping from the roof and going deep on the outskirts of the village. They evaded the butcher and met up with Luxon. They decided to go to the library to meet up with Werola and Cedric and get the hell out of Hoghaven.
 
1. **The White Pox**

Our adventurers left Hoghaven with Werola and Cedric, northbound. Due to their empathy, they decided to return to the village in order to save the rest of the villagers.
 
They observed the library and saw a guard inside it, searching for something. They scurried along and overheard Leo giving orders from the Constabulary. Our party decided to meet Leo head on and ask about the situation. He explained to them that a letter came from Waterdeep, relieving him of his duty. After the brutal murder of one of his guardsmen, Leo felt something was up with Henry and sent a scouting party to investigate the inn. Our party joined them.
 
The scene at the inn was the stuff of nightmares. Mangled body parts scattered under the windows of the inn. Our party gently and thoroughly investigated all entrances for any traps, but there were none. One of the guards grabbed the handle on the door and started feeling very ill. The second guard too. They both started getting a heatstroke until they overheated into death. Azlyn identified it as a version of the White Pox.
 
Grom stormed the inn and spotted a Dragonborn of Tiamat who attacked them. Leo rushed in to help them, but perished in the fight. The Dragonborn nearly slaughtered them all, but fortunately, guards came to rescue our party. Our party then traveled to the blacksmith for a moment of respite.
 
1. **Kharvem's Loyalty**

The party, battered and bruised from the fight with the Dragonborn, travels north towards the library. Outside the library, the corpse of Ben, one of the guards Azlyn met, had been mangled by a blunt force trauma. The party thought the man inside the library was his killer, but it was soon dismissed.
 
They meet up with a mysterious guard called Kharvem who presents himself as one of the guards of House Wyndall. He says that he was ill for a couple of days with a high fever and missed his duty. The party informs him that his guard friends are dead which demoralizes Kharvem. He starts taking off his armour with the crest of House Wyndall, cursing and muttering about how nothing makes sense and how the Wyndalls wish to create a lumbermill in the village, their purpose strange. A 100K trees to construct a flying ship. But the schematics were inside the _A__rcana Obscura_ book, the one he was trying to find.
 
The party gave him an offer he could not refuse, to join them and get revenge on the people who slaughtered his friends. He reluctantly accepted, but finally given a new purpose. They decide to stock up on any information about the locale, about survival books and anything connected to Tiamat. After that, they decided to scavenge weapons and armour before heading out. They pay a visit to the constabulary. Grom finds transactional data in an disjointed mess, Luxon hears voices calling for help, Talia reads the note from Lord Wyndall and finds a message from her brother written in Thieves' Cant all while Azlyn observes. With the main office cleared, they open the armoury and almost meet their doom.
 
Two guardsmen with spiral marks attack them in the armoury, nearly slaughtering them. Luckily, Grom takes one of them out, while the second one gets restrained and interrogated. He mentions The Red Rose, Triboar, becoming a slave to save his family, but then his mind goes blank. Luxon keeps hearing strange voices in his head. Talia starts hearing them too. Grom too. They put the man out of his misery. When he died, three worms appeared out of his head. Grom captures one of them in a jar, while Kharvem stomps the remaining ones. The guards weapon and armour turn to sand as they fall dead. They search the armoury for any weapons and armour, but find nothing special. In one of the chests, Luxon finds a **mysterious green egg** that called to him. The egg thanks him for the rescue, while others look on in shock.
 
The party decides to burn the remains of Ben and the guards they defeated, just to be sure. That prompts Tuli Hardcloak, Gili's wife to yell at them and threaten to put out the fire. Grom calmly explains what the situation is with the Pox and says that this is the best way to get rid of it. Tuli disagrees, but is powerless to stop them. Kharvem asks her where the hells has she been all this time, to which she responds that she is preparing lunch for her husband who went East with Laila.
 
The party continues gathering resources from the village. Azlyn meets with Mesarth and Kharvem where Kharvem mentions that it is time for Order of Bahamut to return. Mesarth nods in agreement, even though it seems too fast to him. Azlyn looks on in confusion.
 
Grom meets with Celorfin and Coins, explaining the situation and persuades them to leave the village with them. They oblige and ready their tools for departure.
 
Talia and Luxon investigate the Inn and scour it for food and resources. Before leaving, Talia remembers the strange ruby eye bracelet Grom had. She takes a closer look and starts hearing voices in her head, speaking in Infernal. Azlyn translates what the voices spoke, and Grom just grabs the bracelet and turns it into dust. The voice lets out a final message to Talia, reminding her that the Sands will reclaim all.
 
1. **T****he Voyage**

Stocked and ready, the party, alongside Kharvem, Mesarth, Celorfin and Coins leave the village, traveling north to their campsite.
