>_This place is a playground, that's what it is. [[Gods]], mortals, and devils playing around. Fools who think they can bend a continent to their will. But Faerûn is never going to bend to their will. Never has. Either they must change to dwell in it, or it will break them, like so many others lost in the void of history._

Faerûn is a contintent on the planet Toril.
The word "Faerûn" was a modified version of "Faerie", the name of the homeland of ancient elves.
The continent includes terrain that is as varied as any other. Besides the exterior coastline to the west and south, the most dominant feature on the continent was the Sea of Fallen Stars. This is an irregular inland sea that kept the interior lands fertile, connected the west and east regions of Faerûn and served as a major trade route for many of the bordering nations.
