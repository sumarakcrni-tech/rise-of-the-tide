>*The dusty parchment reveals scribbles of someone who suffered.*

_Ah, Dilath. Ever the youngest child of a long-forgotten Dawnstar family. Yours was the most noblest of families in [[Waterdeep]], that is until Daleth made sure you were all forgotten. But still, even after he did those atrocities, he was still your brother and you loved him. His darkness soon became your own. His obsessions your own. Always rambling on about restoring true order. You were fascinated by his expirements on those poor villagers in Orogoth. The land twisted and turned in your wake, and you liked it. But it did not last long, because nature had other plans for you. You fell ill seemingly out of nowhere, and the medicine men called it Brain Rot. Your thoughts changed from day to day, but one thing kept you sane, your brother. He wanted you to have a new family. You still remember that night as if it never ended, when your bodies became one. It was the right thing to do. Your brother was happy and you had a family. But that family was soon taken away from you again when the Alliance came down on all of you. You fled with your child up north from Orogoth and made your way into [[the High Forest]]. There, you made your new home with your child. But one child was not enough. You wanted another family. And family you made once more. The orphans loved you with all their hearts. And you loved them back. But loving them was not enough. You wanted, nay, needed to protect them. And so you did it. You made them better, stronger. You made them like you. But their screams still haunt you. You loved them so much, but they did not love you. They never loved you. How could they love a monster like you?_
 
_NO! I AM NOT A MONSTER! I ONLY WANTED TO HAVE A FAMILY! PLEASE! MOTHER! FATHER! DON'T LET HIM TOUCH ME! PLEASE! I BEG OF YOU! NO!_
