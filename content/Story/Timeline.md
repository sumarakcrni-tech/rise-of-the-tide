>*Time is relative, but the stories it carries are not.*

## First of Mirtul, 1489 DR

### The Meeting
- The intelligent machine known only as [[Grom]] met with a cleric woman called [[Azlyn]] near a library where a robbery happened. The guards informed them to speak to [[Werola Thynid]], the local librarian. She explained to [[Grom]] that pages were torn from the books which was odd, and another book called _[[Arcana Obscura]]_ was stolen which seemed to really upset her. [[Grom]] and [[Azlyn]] promised to help her find the book that was stolen, for a price, of course. She agreed and pointed them east bound.
 
- In front of the constabulary, they met a strange man called [[Luxon]] who had just arrived to town. [[Grom]] and [[Azlyn]] did not interact much with him, but they had the same idea. They proceeded to visit the Constabulary ran by [[Leo Cartwright]] to obtain more information about the recent attack. He provided little information, saying only that bandits were spotted on the [[Eastern Road]]. He did however, offer a bounty prize for the bandits. [[Grom]], [[Azlyn]] and [[Luxon]] accepted it, which led to bickering which was resolved by agreeing to split the bounty between them. [[Grom]] wandered around the constabulary and silently observed [[Leo Cartwright]]'s assistant, [[Laila Farlight]]. She told him to get back and leave her alone, to which [[Grom]] obliged, but not before noticing that she had a strange spiral tattoo on her left hand and some strange papers she was looking at.
 
- [[Leo Cartwright]] remembered there had been a strange occurrence in the village, where a hog from the [[Cedric Cleves]] Family Farm had been stolen, which the former blamed on his rival family, The Hardcloaks. They agreed to investigate. The first place to start would be the local tavern.
 
- On their way to the tavern, [[Azlyn]] wished to talk to the Cleves Family members, but was swiftly rejected. She did notice that there were no windows on the little hovel the family was situated. [[Grom]] decided to stop by the local church where he encountered a dragonkin acolyte called [[Mesarth]]. They chatted about the missing book and [[Werola Thynid]], but he did not hear anything. He did say that what happened was awful and he had hoped if [[Grom]] could retrieve the stolen books so [[Mesarth]] could organize a book reading competition. [[Grom]] reluctantly agreed. With [[Azlyn]] and [[Luxon]] already gone, [[Grom]] continued on towards the tavern, but sidetracked when he spotted a sign saying _Smithy_.
 
- The machine entered the shop and met [[Celorfin Garaitnu]], an elven blacksmith with his [[Kenku]] apprentice, [[Coins]]. They chatted about the recent bandit attacks, but he did not hear anything. [[Grom]] asked for some help in his blacksmithing endeavors and [[Celorfin Garaitnu]] obliged. After a couple of tips and tricks, [[Grom]] thanked the man and left the smithy for the tavern.

### The Tavern
- Inside the tavern they met a strange elven woman that was performing in front a crowd of guardsmen. The crowd cheered on for her as she continued to enchant them with a tambourine/drum combo that left the crowd roaring for more. No lasting effort on our adventurers, though. [[Grom]], [[Azlyn]] and [[Luxon]] sat at an empty table and the elven woman joined them. She introduced herself as [[Talia]] Wyndall. They chatted about the missing book that [[Werola Thynid]] mentioned and the reward for returning it, which prompted [[Talia]] to ask to join them. After a round of drinks, they proceeded into the village to explore.
 
- When they got out of the tavern, [[Luxon]] noticed something strange. A voice spoke to him inside his head. Mentioning the [[Eastern Road]]. He noticed a small particle of dried-up blood on the floor near the tavern, and deducted it was the blood of a dragonkin. This did seem rather strange to everyone around him. He ignored them and they proceeded together to to square where a circus had set up shop.
 
- They talked to the merchants who offered them their wares, when they were interrupted by a dwarven man called [[Gili Hardcloak]]. He spoke how [[Cedric Cleves]] blames his family for the missing hog, which was absurd nonsense and asks the adventurers to help clear his name. They agree to investigate, but not before [[Talia]] notices that he has a strange tattoo on his right hand. They investigated the Cleves Farm for any signs of trespass, but found none. [[Talia]] caught eye of a strange cart sitting outside the Library, and decided to take a sneak peek into the cart. [[Azlyn]] followed. They found three crates shut tight and tried to open them, but to no avail. One of the guards from the Library came out to guard it, but they slipped past him, their cover intact.
 
- [[Luxon]], on the other hand, investigated the pig pen of the Hardcloak family when he heard voices calling to him. Somebody, or something, was fed to the pigs of the Hardcloak family. The news were disturbing.

### Travel Eastwards
- The adventurers continued talking to the merchants, when a ring caught [[Talia]]'s eye. Being the thief that she is, she pocketed it, creating a minor illusion in its place. The merchant never noticed the difference, and when he did, they were already long gone. Their decision was to proceed into the [[Eastern Road]]. On the road, the group ended up passing by a caravan that they deemed strange looking, with odd masks donning their faces. 

- The group continued on the road, ending up going towards [[The High Forest]] on a downtrodden path. [[Grom]] ended up stepping on makeshift spikes, forcing the group to rethink their approach. [[Talia]] and [[Luxon]] proposed going slowly and searching for traps, which [[Grom]] agreed with. Something moved in the deep forest and started skittering towards them. Nobody could see what it was, but it sounded like something massive. The promptly turned around and went back to [[Hoghaven]]. 

- After a couple of hours, the adventurers returned, battered and bruised and rested in the inn.

## Second of Mirtul, 1489 DR

### The Mystery Deepens
- [[Azlyn]] and [[Talia]] woke up at night. Something was dripping from one of the cupboards. Blood. They called for [[Grom]] and [[Luxon]] who investigated what was inside the cupboard. A head of a dead pig. This was problematic. The head had been magically clotted to stop it from bleeding until a certain time.

- They moved out the tavern and into the dimly lit roads of [[Hoghaven]]. [[Talia]] spotted a guard near the cart next to the Library which seemed odd. The team planned an elaborate heist from the Constabulary to steal the strange papers [[Grom]] noticed.

- [[Talia]] went scouting and found a place of entry to the constabulary. The back window. She noticed a single guard awake next to an elven prisoner. The plan quickly fell into chaos as [[Talia]] slipped while entering through the window. The guard did not spot her, but went to wake up the other guards. [[Talia]] quickly went in and lockpicked the gate, allowing the [[elf]] to open the portcullis and quickly escape. She soon realized that it was [[Cedric Cleves]] who she had rescued. They regrouped with the rest of the team, going with him into his house.

### The Hogs of [[Hoghaven]]
- [[Cedric Cleves]] spoke about his missing hog, to which [[Talia]] created an illusion of the hog head that was found in the inn. [[Cedric Cleves]] fell into tears and anger as he saw his beloved hog, now confirmed dead and slain. They spoke about how [[Laila Farlight]] wants to overthrow [[Leo Cartwright]] from the Constabulary, how Hardcloaks brand their pigs on the tongue and how much he hates the aristocracy. He gave them a bunch of strange notes that appeared to be transactional data with the timeline when a caravan appeared in the city, about a month ago. He did not know why his hog was branded by the Hardcloaks only to be slaughtered.

- [[Grom]] suggested to escort [[Cedric Cleves]] out of the city, since he was a wanted man. [[Cedric Cleves]] said that he wishes to visit [[Werola Thynid]] as she is the only person he can trust. [[Azlyn]] and [[Talia]] offered to disguise him before they went to the library to avoid drawing attention. They went out into the night, slowly creeping towards the library, avoiding the guards. [[Werola Thynid]] was half asleep when they knocked on her door. She opened them because of [[Grom]]. They explained the whole situation to her and she decided to help them hide Cedric in the Library. She gave our adventurers a map of Faerun and hid underneath the giant table with Cedric. Cedric informed them that Laila resides in the inn, the first room on the right.

- Our party recalled the guard that was guarding the cart near the library, and they really wanted to know what he was hiding. They devised a plan which included creating an iron ring that would be placed on the guard using a mage hand, and [[Grom]] would then shock the guard, sending him into a paralysis. The plan worked flawlessly. The guard collapsed and they went to loot the crates. They found 4 strange robes with a wooden mask, 4 silver bracelets and a ruby eye bracelet. Underneath that, a small wooden necklace lay. [[Azlyn]] recognized it as one of the [[Silvan]]'s gifts. In the final crate, they found a **mysterious egg**, warm to the touch.

- The party proceeded to the inn where they were greeted by the Innkeeper, [[Henry Serpentwind]]. They went to [[Talia]]'s room, but suddenly, they got locked in. They tried every way to get out, until [[Grom]] decided enough was enough. He stormed out of the room, shattering the door in the process. He was greeted by two guards, mistaking him for [[Leo Cartwright]]. [[Grom]] persuaded them to go downstairs with him and apologize to the Innkeeper, and to pay for repairs. [[Grom]] explained the situation to the Innkeeper, and soon learned that the previous owner of the room was [[Leo Cartwright]]. The pieces were falling into place.

- Meanwhile, up on the second floor, [[Talia]] decided to do a little sneak peek into [[Laila Farlight]]'s room. She picked her lock, only to be greeted by [[Laila Farlight]], who closed the door and locked it again. [[Talia]] and [[Luxon]] then decided to see if there was a way to spy on her from the room next door. [[Talia]] went up on the roof and observed the room through the window. She created a distraction with a foul smell which forced her to get out of the room. [[Talia]] then swiftly entered the room and rummaged through [[Laila Farlight]]'s possessions, finding a note with 4 names, [[Laila Farlight]], [[Rulf Redstream]], [[Henry Serpentwind]] and Hardcloaks, also, a note speaking of meeting with someone west.

- [[Grom]] went upstairs and met up with the rest of the party, when they decided to get out. [[Luxon]] went down the stairs first and nearly perished. The warm and welcoming mask of the Innkeeper fell as he opened the back door where a mountain of man rushed towards the guards that were still down there and planted his meat cleaver between his eyes.  

- [[Luxon]] ejected himself from the window, running away from the Butcher [[Rulf Redstream]].

- [[Azlyn]], [[Grom]] and [[Talia]] decided to leave the inn ASAP, jumping from the roof and going deep on the outskirts of the village. They evaded the butcher and met up with [[Luxon]]. They decided to go to the library to meet up with [[Werola Thynid]] and [[Cedric Cleves]] and get the hell out of [[Hoghaven]].

## Third of Mirtul, 1489 DR

### The [[White Pox]]
- Our adventurers left [[Hoghaven]] with [[Werola Thynid]] and [[Cedric Cleves]], northbound. Due to their empathy, they decided to return to the village in order to save the rest of the villagers.
 
- They observed the library and saw a guard inside it, searching for something. They scurried along and overheard [[Leo Cartwright]] giving orders from the Constabulary. Our party decided to meet [[Leo Cartwright]] head on and ask about the situation. He explained to them that a letter came from [[Waterdeep]], relieving him of his duty. After the brutal murder of one of his guardsmen, Leo felt something was up with [[Henry Serpentwind]] and sent a scouting party to investigate the inn. Our party joined them.
 
- The scene at the inn was the stuff of nightmares. Mangled body parts scattered under the windows of the inn. Our party slowly and thoroughly investigated all entrances for any traps, but there were none. One of the guards grabbed the handle on the door and started feeling very ill. The second guard too. They both started getting a heatstroke until they overheated into death. [[Azlyn]] identified it as a version of the [[White Pox]].
 
- [[Grom]] stormed the inn and spotted a [[Dragonborn]] of Tiamat who attacked them. [[Leo Cartwright]] rushed in to help them, but perished in the fight. The [[Dragonborn]] nearly slaughtered them all, but fortunately, guards came to rescue our party. Our party then traveled to the blacksmith for a moment of respite.

### Loyalty
- The party, battered and bruised from the fight with the [[Dragonborn]], travels north towards the library. Outside the library, the corpse of Ben, one of the guards [[Azlyn]] met, had been mangled by a blunt force trauma. The party thought the man inside the library was his killer, but it was soon dismissed.
 
- They meet up with a mysterious guard called [[Kharvem Shog]] who presents himself as one of the guards of [[House Wyndall]]. He says that he was ill for a couple of days with a high fever and missed his duty. The party informs him that his guard friends are dead which demoralizes [[Kharvem Shog]]. He starts taking off his armor with the crest of [[House Wyndall]], cursing and muttering about how nothing makes sense and how the Wyndalls wish to create a lumbermill in the village, their purpose strange. 100K trees to construct a flying ship. But the schematics were inside the [[Arcana Obscura]] book, the one he was trying to find.
 
- The party gave him an offer he could not refuse, to join them and get revenge on the people who slaughtered his friends. He reluctantly accepted, but finally given a new purpose. They decide to stock up on any information about the locale, about survival books and anything connected to Tiamat. After that, they decided to scavenge weapons and armor before heading out. They pay a visit to the constabulary. [[Grom]] finds transactional data in an disjointed mess, [[Luxon]] hears voices calling for help, [[Talia]] reads the note from Lord [[House Wyndall|Wyndall]] and finds a message from her brother written in Thieves' Cant all while [[Azlyn]] observes the situation. With the main office cleared, they open the armory and almost meet their doom.
 
- Two guardsmen with spiral marks attack them in the armory, nearly slaughtering them. Luckily, [[Grom]] takes one of them out, while the second one gets restrained and interrogated. He mentions [[The Flaming Rose Company]], [[Triboar]], becoming a slave to save his family, but then his mind goes blank. [[Luxon]] keeps hearing strange voices in his head. [[Talia]] starts hearing them too. [[Grom]] too. They put the man out of his misery. When he died, three worms appeared out of his head. [[Grom]] captures one of them in a jar, while [[Kharvem Shog]] stomps the remaining ones. The guards weapon and armor turn to sand as they fall dead. They search the armory for any weapons and armor, but find nothing special. In one of the chests, [[Luxon]] finds a **mysterious green egg** that called to him. The egg thanks him for the rescue, while others look on in shock.
 
- The party decides to burn the remains of Ben and the guards they defeated, just to be sure. That prompts [[Turi Hardcloak]], [[Gili Hardcloak]]'s wife to yell at them and threaten to put out the fire. [[Grom]] calmly explains what the situation is with the [[White Pox]] and says that this is the best way to get rid of it. Tuli disagrees, but is powerless to stop them. Kharvem asks her where the hells has she been all this time, to which she responds that she is preparing lunch for her husband who went East with Laila.
 
-  The party continues gathering resources from the village. [[Azlyn]] meets with [[Mesarth]] and [[Kharvem Shog]] where [[Kharvem Shog]] mentions that it is time for [[Order of Bahamut]] to return. [[Mesarth]] nods in agreement, even though it seems too fast to him. [[Azlyn]] looks on in confusion.
 
- [[Grom]] meets with [[Celorfin Garaitnu|Celorfin]] and [[Coins]], explaining the situation and persuades them to leave the village with them. They oblige and ready their tools for departure.
 
- [[Talia]] and [[Luxon]] investigate the Inn and scour it for food and resources. Before leaving, [[Talia]] remembers the strange ruby eye bracelet [[Grom]] had. She takes a closer look and starts hearing voices in her head, speaking in Infernal. [[Azlyn]] translates what the voices spoke, and [[Grom]] just grabs the bracelet and turns it into dust. The voice lets out a final message to [[Talia]], reminding her that the Sands will reclaim all.
 
## Fourth of Mirtul, 1489 DR

### The Campsite
- Stocked and ready, the party, alongside [[Kharvem Shog]], [[Mesarth]], [[Celorfin Garaitnu|Celorfin]] and [[Coins]] leave the village, traveling north to their campsite.

- As our adventurers leave [[Hoghaven]] with the resources they scavanged, [[Luxon]] starts hearing voices in his head, calling him for help, saying to go to the Eastern Cave. Luckily, the party spots the cave on the local map they found in the Constabulary. After a couple of minutes bickering, [[Luxon]] and [[Talia]] decide that the party should stop there, just in case.
 
- They stop at the cave entrance and hear voices coming from the inside. [[Luxon]] and [[Talia]] manage to evade the carefully laid out trap and observe the shattered pieces of a large statue. Meanwhile, [[Azlyn]] tries to follow them, but triggers the trap, causing a bunch of rocks to fall on her, knocking her unconscious. [[Grom]], already frustrated with [[Azlyn]]'s inability, stabilizes her and throws her on the cart.
 
- [[Talia]] and [[Luxon]] proceed deeper into the cave, hearing a voice calling for help. But before they can get there, [[Luxon]] spots another trap that [[Talia]] disarms with her Mage Hand. They rush towards the wounded wood [[elf]] and call for [[Mesarth]] to provide the necessary aid. He flusters around but [[Talia]] calms him. He heals the wounded wood [[elf]] who falls unconscious. They grab the wounded [[elf]] and her dead partner and place them on the cart and continue towards their campsite.
 
- Back at the campsite, [[Werola Thynid|Werola]] and [[Cedric Cleves|Cedric]] prepare a campfire and store the food they scavenged. But suddenly, a blue light appears from the ground. The party arrives at the campsite, but ignores the light. They start cutting wood to build a simple shelter for the night.
 
- But then, they start hearing footsteps from the [High Forest](The%20High%20Forest.md). Eyes glowing in the dark, between the trees. The wooden footsteps draw ever closer, and the party prepares for a fight. But suddenly, the footsteps stop and retreat back into the woods. [[Talia]] determines it is possible that those were Wood Woads. [[Grom]] noticed something strange on one of the trees. A spiral marking.
 
- As the night continues, the sounds can be heard again coming from the forest, but never come closer. The wood [[elf]] called Wynra woke up. She thanked them for rescuing her and spoke about how she and Lyza fled a shadowy creature into the cave where we found them. By some strange coincidence, or perhaps fate, the campsite our party went to was near one of wood elves settlements, in a cave below ground level. The party asked about those strange spiral markings on the trees, and Wynra mentioned [[the Flaming Rose Company]] and how they are the ones doing that. To what end, nobody knows.
 
- Wynra asks the party if they are willing to join her in venturing in the cave underground, but [[Grom]] and [[Azlyn]] say no. [[Talia]] and [[Luxon]], on the other hand, accept her proposal and venture with her underground. Deep in the cave, they find [[Sylzana the Faithful|Sylzana]] who claims she is their leader. Sylzana informs the party about missing wood elves in the [High Forest](The%20High%20Forest.md). A scouting party noticed a strange caravan going through the forest and moving north. A couple of days later, strange markings started appearing on the trees. She also talks about their deity called [[Silvan]] and the whole group of them chants *Nature is balance. Balance is life. In [[Silvan]], we are one*.
