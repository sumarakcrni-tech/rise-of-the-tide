>*Time is relative, but the stories it carries are not.*

## First of Mirtul, 1489 DR

### The Meeting
- The intelligent machine known only as [[Grom]] met with a cleric woman called [[Azlyn Bennett]] near a library where a robbery happened. The guards informed them to speak to [[Werola Thynid]]l, the local librarian. She explained to [[Grom]] that pages were torn from the books which was odd, and another book called _Arcana Obscura_ was stolen which seemed to really upset her. [[Grom]] and Azlyn promised to help her find the book that was stolen, for a price, of course. She agreed and pointed them east bound.
 
- In front of the constabulary, they met a strange man called [[Luxon]] who had just arrived to town. [[Grom]] and [[Azlyn Bennett]] did not interact much with him, but they had the same idea. They proceeded to visit the Constabulary ran by [[Leo Cartwright]] to obtain more information about the recent attack. He provided little information, saying only that bandits were spotted on the Eastern Road. He did however, offer a bounty prize for the bandits. [[Grom]], [[Azlyn Bennett]] and [[Luxon]] accepted it, which led to bickering which was resolved by agreeing to split the bounty between them. [[Grom]] wandered around the constabulary and silently observed [[Leo Cartwright]]'s assistant, [[Laila Farlight]]. She told him to get back and leave her alone, to which [[Grom]] obliged, but not before noticing that she had a strange spiral tattoo on her left hand and some strange papers she was looking at.
 
- [[Leo Cartwright]] remembered there had been a strange occurrence in the village, where a hog from the [[Cedric Cleves]] Family Farm had been stolen, which the former blamed on his rival family, The Hardcloaks. They agreed to investigate. The first place to start would be the local tavern.
 
- On their way to the tavern, [[Azlyn Bennett]] wished to talk to the Cleves Family members, but was swiftly rejected. She did notice that there were no windows on the little hovel the family was situated. [[Grom]] decided to stop by the local church where he encountered a dragonkin acolyte called [[Mesarth]]. They chatted about the missing book and [[Werola Thynid]], but he did not hear anything. He did say that what happened was awful and he had hoped if [[Grom]] could retrieve the stolen books so [[Mesarth]] could organize a book reading competition. [[Grom]] reluctantly agreed. With [[Azlyn Bennett]] and [[Luxon]] already gone, [[Grom]] continued on towards the tavern, but sidetracked when he spotted a sign saying _Smithy_.
 
- The machine entered the shop and met [[Celorfin Garaitnu]], an elven blacksmith with his [[Kenku]] apprentice, [[Coins]]. They chatted about the recent bandit attacks, but he did not hear anything. [[Grom]] asked for some help in his blacksmithing endevours and [[Celorfin Garaitnu]] obliged. After a couple of tips and tricks, [[Grom]] thanked the man and left the smithy for the tavern.

### The Tavern

- Inside the tavern they met a strange elven woman that was performing in front a crowd of guardsmen. The crowd cheered on for her as she continued to enchant them with a tamburine/drum combo that left the crowd roaring for more. No lasting effort on our adventurers, though. [[Grom]], [[Azlyn Bennett]] and [[Luxon]] sat at an empty table and the elven woman joined them. She introduced herself as [[Talia]] Wyndall. They chatted about the missing book that [[Werola Thynid]] mentioned and the reward for returning it, which prompted [[Talia]] to ask to join them. After a round of drinks, they proceeded into the village to explore.
 
- When they got out of the tavern, [[Luxon]] noticed something strange. A voice spoke to him inside his head. Mentioning the Eastern Road. He noticed a small particle of dried-up blood on the floor near the tavern, and deducted it was the blood of a dragonkin. This did seem rather strange to everyone around him. He ignored them and they proceeded together to to square where a circus had set up shop.
 
- They talked to the merchants who offered them their wares, when they were interrupted by a dwarven man called [[Gili Hardcloak]]. He spoke how [[Cedric Cleves]] blames his family for the missing hog, which was absurd nonsense and asks the adventurers to help clear his name. They agree to investigate, but not before [[Talia]] notices that he has a strange tattoo on his right hand. They investigated the Cleves Farm for any signs of trespass, but found none. [[Talia]] caught eye of a strange cart sitting outside the Library, and decided to take a sneak peek into the cart. [[Azlyn Bennett]] followed. They found three crates shut tight and tried to open them, but to no avail. One of the guards from the Library came out to guard it, but they slipped past him, their cover intact.
 
- [[Luxon]], on the other hand, investigated the pig pen of the Hardcloak family when he heard voices calling to him. Somebody, or something, was fed to the pigs of the Hardcloak family. The news were disturbing.

### Travel Eastwards
- The adventurers continued talking to the merchants, when a ring caught [[Talia]]'s eye. Being the thief that she is, she pocketed it, creating a minor illusion in its place. The merchant never noticed the difference, and when he did, they were already long gone. Their decision was to proceed into the Eastern Road. On the road, the group ended up passing by a caravan that they deemed strange looking, with odd masks donning their faces. 
- The group continued on the road, ending up going towards [[The High Forest]] on a downtrodden path. [[Grom]] ended up stepping on makeshift spikes, forcing the group to rethink their approach. [[Talia]] and [[Luxon]] proposed going slowly and searching for traps, which [[Grom]] agreed with. Something moved in the deep forest and started skittering towards them. Nobody could see what it was, but it sounded like something massive. The promptly turned around and went back to [[Hoghaven]]. 
- After a couple of hours, the adventurers returned, battered and bruised and rested in the inn.

## Second of Mirtul, 1489 DR

### The Mystery Deepens
- [[Azlyn Bennett]] and [[Talia]] woke up at night. Something was dripping from one of the cupboards. Blood. They called for [[Grom]] and [[Luxon]] who investigated what was inside the cupboard. A head of a dead pig. The news was troubling. The head had been magically clotted to stop it from bleeding until a certain time.
- They moved out the tavern and into the dimly lit roads of [[Hoghaven]]. [[Talia]] spotted a guard near the cart next to the Library which seemed odd. The team planned an elaborate heist from the Constabulary to steal the strange papers [[Grom]] noticed.
- [[Talia]] went scouting and found a place of entry to the constabulary. The back window. She noticed a single guard awake next to an elven prisoner. The plan quickly fell into chaos as [[Talia]] slipped while entering through the window. The guard did not spot her, but went to wake up the other guards. [[Talia]] quickly went in and lockpicked the gate, allowing the [[elf]] to open the portcullis and quickly escape. She soon realized that it was [[Cedric Cleves]] who she had rescued. They regrouped with the rest of the team, going with him into his house.

### The Hogs of [[Hoghaven]]

- Cedric spoke about his missing hog, to which [[Talia]] created an illusion of the hog head that was found in the inn. Cedric fell into tears and anger as he saw his beloved hog, now confirmed dead and slain. They spoke about how Laila wants to overthrow Leo from the Constabulary, how Hardcloaks brand their pigs on the tongue and how much he hates the aristocracy. He gave them a bunch of strange notes that appearred to be transactional data with the timeline when a caravan appeared in the city, about a month ago. He did not know why his hog was branded by the Hardcloaks only to be slaughtered.
- [[Grom]] suggested to escort Cedric out of the city, since he was a wanted man. Cedric said that he wishes to visit Werola as she is the only person he can trust. Azlyn and [[Talia]] offered to disguise him before they went to the library to avoid drawing attention. They went out into the night, slowly creeping towards the library, avoiding the guards. Werola was half asleep when they knocked on her door. She opened them because of [[Grom]]. They explained the whole situation to her and she decided to help them hide Cedric in the Library. She gave our adventurers a map of Faerun and hid underneath the giant table with Cedric. Cedric informed them that Laila resides in the inn, the first room on the right.
- Our party recalled the guard that was guarding the cart near the library, and they really wanted to know what he was hiding. They devised a plan which included creating an iron ring that would be placed on the guard using a mage hand, and [[Grom]] would then shock the guard, sending him into a paralysis. The plan worked flawlessly. The guard collapsed and they went to loot the crates. They found 4 strange robes with a wooden mask, 4 silver bracelets and a ruby eye bracelet. Underneath that, a small wooden necklace lay. Azlyn recognized it as one of the Leshen's gifts. In the final crate, they found a **mysterious egg**, warm to the touch.
- The party proceeded to the inn where they were greeted by the Innkeeper, [[Henry Serpentwind]]. They went to [[Talia]]'s room, but suddenly, they got locked in. They tried every way to get out, until [[Grom]] decided enough was enough. He stormed out of the room, shattering the door in the process. He was greeted by two guards, mistaking him for [[Leo Cartwright]]. [[Grom]] persuaded them to go downstairs with him and apologize to the Innkeeper, and to pay for repairs. [[Grom]] explained the situation to the Innkeeper, and soon learned that the previous owner of the room was [[Leo Cartwright]]. The pieces were falling into place.
- Meanwhile, up on the second floor, [[Talia]] decided to do a little sneak peek into Laila's room. She picked her lock, only to be greeted by Laila, who closed the door and locked it again. [[Talia]] and [[Luxon]] then decided to see if there was a way to spy on her from the room next door. [[Talia]] went up on the roof and observed the room through the window. She created a distraction with a foul smell which forced Laila to get out of the room. [[Talia]] then swiftly entered the room and rummaged through Laila's possessions, finding a note with 4 names, Laila, Rulf, Henry and Hardcloak, also, a note speaking of meeting with someone West.
- [[Grom]] went upstairs and met up with the rest of the party, when they decided to get out. [[Luxon]] went down the stairs first and nearly perished. The warm and welcoming mask of the Innkeeper fell as he opened the back door where a mountain of man rushed towards the guards that were still down there and planted his meat cleaver between his eyes.  
- [[Luxon]] ejected himself from the window, running away from the Butcher Rulf.
- Azlyn, [[Grom]] and [[Talia]] decided to leave the inn ASAP, jumping from the roof and going deep on the outskirts of the village. They evaded the butcher and met up with [[Luxon]]. They decided to go to the library to meet up with Werola and Cedric and get the hell out of [[Hoghaven]].
