Nestled south of [[Secomber]] lie the Ruins of Gloom. Once known as Selren Themar, a place of prosperity, knowledge, and beauty, now a poisonous ruin of swamp and mud. The once elven settlement has been long reduced to dust.  
The ruins are now inhabited by strange creatures, known only as Shad'rai to outsiders. They are the worshippers of [[Shar]].
 
Elven women guardians are all brainwashed survivors of Selren Themar.  
Their lower jaws have been severed and implanted again with spikes and magic.  
Controlled through their helmets by Erethas, located in Orogoth.
