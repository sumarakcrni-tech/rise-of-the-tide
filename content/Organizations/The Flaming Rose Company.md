>*The Empire shall rise once again! The Shadovars will not be forgotten!*

It is a network of mercenaries, spies and wizards who worship the Shade.
 
Founded in 1486 DR in the [[Netherese]] flying city of Thultanthar by Noress the Artist, the organizations main goal is to find obscure, magical items that would aid the [[Netherese]] empire.
 
Following the fall of Thultanthar after the war with Cormyr, the organization rebranded itself as a trading company. Now, they scurry over Faerun in search of magical items that would restore the [[Netherese]] empire!

# **P****lot Points**

The Flaming Rose Company hired a group of adventureres to escort a shipment from the South into [[Triboar]]. Led by Grandmaster Thoray and Hardir Braveson, the caravan stops for a night on the outskirts of [[Red Larch]]. The adventureres clear out the area of roaming beasts and the caravan continues. But, little do they know what truly hides underneath those crates they are delivering. The Eye of Shad.
