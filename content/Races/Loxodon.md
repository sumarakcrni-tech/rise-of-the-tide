The humanoid elephants called loxodons are often oases of calm in the busy streets of south [[Faerûn]]. They hum or chant in sonorous tones and move slowly or sit in perfect stillness. If provoked to action, loxodons are true terrors—bellowing with rage, trumpeting and flapping their ears. Their serene wisdom, fierce loyalty, and unwavering conviction are tremendous assets to their guilds.

Loxodons typically stayed with their own kind, but would travel to cities for trade. They ignored the conflicts of the region around them unless it threatened their own people. To them, extremes in life were detrimental, whereas balance contributed to the survival of the herd. Although most Loxodons on Toril originally lived in the Shaar, some could also be found around the region of the Golden Water. By the 15th century DR, they could also be found further west in the Chultan Peninsula.
