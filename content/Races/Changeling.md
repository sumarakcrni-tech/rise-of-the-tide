>*Changelings are a fey or humanoid race related to doppelgangers who had a natural ability for shapeshifting, making them effective as actors, spies, and criminals.*

When in their true or natural form, a changeling looked rather like a doppelganger, with a lesser resemblance to a regular humanoid; in comparison to a [[human]], they appeared faded and lacking detail and might be mistaken for someone with albinism. A typical changeling had skin of a pale grey hue, thin hair of a light silvery shade, and large eyes that were blank white. Their nose and mouth were small, even barely noticeable, so they seemed expressionless and almost featureless. In contrast to doppelgangers, changelings displayed sexual characteristics in their natural form, though could alter these as they pleased. A changeling had a humanoid build some 5 feet and ​3 inches​ to ​5 feet and ​9 inches (160​ to ​180 centimeters) in height, but seemed gangly and ill-proportioned, having limbs that were slender and somewhat longer than a similarly sized humanoid. Some could change their height from [[human]] to [[Halfling]] size. Despite this, they did not lack for grace or agility. Of course, given their shapeshifting talents, changelings were rarely seen like this.

Changelings are subtle and deceptive in their ways. How a changeling chose and used each new guise they adopted was up to each individual. It might be for mischief, for entertainment, or for heroism, or it could be for terrible misdeeds. To one changeling, it might be no more than a disguise, but to another, it could be another facet of their soul.
